# Questions to ask your pediatrician

*Print this, circle the questions that matter to you, and bring it to your child's next visit. You don't have to ask them all.*

> **This is not medical advice.** It's a list of questions to help you talk with your child's doctor. Only a professional who meets your child can answer them for your family.

**Child's age:** __________ months  **Date of visit:** __________

## Before the visit: my notes

What worries me (what I see, when, how often):

______________________________________________________________

______________________________________________________________

Examples or short videos I can show the doctor: ______________________

What my child is good at and enjoys: ____________________________

## About my child's development

- [ ] Is my child's **talking and understanding** where you'd expect for their age?
- [ ] How does my child's **play, pointing, eye contact and response to their name** look to you?
- [ ] What skills would you expect to see **in the next few months**?
- [ ] Is there anything you'd like me to **watch for** between now and the next visit?
- [ ] Has my child's **hearing** been checked? Should it be checked again?
- [ ] Should my child's **vision** be checked?

## About screening

- [ ] Has my child had a **developmental screening** or an **autism screening** questionnaire? What did it show?
- [ ] What does a positive (or clear) screening result **mean**, and what doesn't it mean?
- [ ] If the screen is clear but I'm still worried, **what's the next step**?

## About evaluation and support

- [ ] Would a **full evaluation** be helpful? Who does it, and how long is the wait?
- [ ] *(US, under 3)* Can you help me contact our **early intervention** program, or can I refer my child myself?
- [ ] *(US, 3 and older)* Should I ask our **public school district** for an evaluation?
- [ ] *(Outside the US)* What **free local early support** is there, and can I refer myself?
- [ ] While we wait, **what can we do** at home?
- [ ] Is there **anything I should not wait** on?

## About screens and daily life

- [ ] How do our family's **screen habits** fit with my child's development? Is there anything you'd suggest we change?
- [ ] I've read about **"virtual autism"**. What do you think about the research? *(It is a term some clinicians use; it is not a medical diagnosis.)*
- [ ] My child uses a **tablet or app to communicate**. How should we think about that? *(Communication devices are not "screen time".)*
- [ ] How are my child's **sleep, eating and activity**, and do they affect any of this?

## About us as a family

- [ ] Where can we find **support and information for our family** while we learn more?
- [ ] If my child is autistic, where can we **learn from autistic adults** and autistic-led groups?
- [ ] Who should I **call** if things change or I have new worries before the next visit?

## After the visit

Next steps: ______________________________________________________

Referrals made (name, phone, date): _________________________________

Follow up by (date): __________  If I haven't heard by then, I will: ____________

---

*You know your child best. Asking questions is a caring step, and it's not your fault if you have worries.*

**Outside the US:** ask your doctor, nurse or health visitor about free local early support.
