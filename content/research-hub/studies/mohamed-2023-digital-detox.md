## In plain language

Researchers in Egypt ran a digital-detox program with 105 middle-school students from September 2021 to February 2022. After the program, the share with high screen "addiction" scores fell from 20.0% to 14.3%, and moderate from 65.7% to 43.8%. This is the main peer-reviewed use of the "electronic screen syndrome" label we found. It concerns adolescents and screen overuse, not autism.

## Who was studied

105 middle-school students, purposive sample, Egypt.

## What was found

- Lower self-reported overuse scores after the program.

## Limitations

- No control group.
- Self-report.
- Purposive sample.

## What it does NOT show

- Anything about autism or young children.
- That the program itself produced the change.
