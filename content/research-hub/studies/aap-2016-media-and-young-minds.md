## In plain language

The American Academy of Pediatrics' 2016 statement for children under 5 is recalled as advising: under 18 months, avoid screen media other than video chatting; at 18 to 24 months, if parents introduce media, choose high-quality programmes and watch together; at 2 to 5 years, about 1 hour a day of high-quality programming, watched together; screen-free mealtimes, bedrooms and parent-child playtime; and a family media plan [VERIFY all against the statement]. Secondary sources report that the AAP's January 2026 "Digital Ecosystems" statement replaced its earlier media guidance. We therefore describe this as the 2016 guidance, not current AAP policy.
