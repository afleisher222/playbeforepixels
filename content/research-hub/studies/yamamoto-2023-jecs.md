## In plain language

Japanese researchers followed 57,980 children and asked which way the link runs: does more TV and DVD viewing come before lower developmental scores, or do lower scores come before more viewing? More TV/DVD time at ages 1 and 2 went with lower developmental screening scores at 2 and 3. Lower developmental scores also went with later increases in screen time among children whose mothers had psychological distress. The journal published a correction to the paper's Key Points.

## Who was studied

57,980 children and mothers, Japan Environment and Children's Study (mothers recruited 2011-2014).

## What was found

- More TV/DVD time at 1 and 2 associated with lower developmental screening scores a year later.
- Lower scores associated with more later screen time where mothers had psychological distress.

## Limitations

- TV/DVD only, no phones or tablets.
- Parent-reported ASQ-3 screener.
- Quote the corrected Key Points.

## What it does NOT show

- Anything about autism.
- One-way causation. The pattern ran both ways in some families.
