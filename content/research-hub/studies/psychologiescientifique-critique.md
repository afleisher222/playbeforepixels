## In plain language

Judging from its title and search listing, this French post argues that the widely shared 2017 alert about screens and autism had no scientific basis. A French search summary that mixed this post with other French sources carried arguments like these: one physician's observations cannot establish how common something is or why it happens; autistic children may be drawn to screens, so an association does not show which came first; and a well-known French researcher on screen effects said that equating screen effects with autism was not justified. We cannot yet tie any of those specific points to this page.

## Who was studied

Not a study. A response to the Ducanda video and the "autisme virtuel" claims in France.

## What was found

- Argues, from its title, that the alert lacked a scientific basis. Its specific arguments must be read on the page before we quote any.
