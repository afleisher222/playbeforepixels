## In plain language

This Indian review describes "virtual autism" as a set of signs from heavy device use, such as social withdrawal, communication problems, inactivity and attention difficulties, which it says are often reversible. It recommends screen limits, age-appropriate content, physical activity, in-person relationships, digital literacy and policy action.

## Who was studied

No new data.

## What was found

- Recommends screen limits and more in-person, active play.

## Limitations

- Not a systematic review.
- Presents the concept as established without weighing the quality of the evidence or the possibility of reverse causation.
- Its claim that the signs are "often reversible" is not supported by controlled studies.

## What it does NOT show

- That screens produce autism-like signs, or that such signs go away when screens are reduced.
