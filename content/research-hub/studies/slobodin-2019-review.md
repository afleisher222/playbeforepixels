## In plain language

This review found that autistic children and adolescents are exposed to more screen time than their peers and start younger. What they watch and who they watch with (with parents or alone) may shape the behaviors linked with exposure. The review noted that the correlates and long-term consequences of screen exposure before age 3 remained largely unexamined.

## Who was studied

Studies of screen use in autistic children and adolescents.

## What was found

- More, and earlier, screen use among autistic children and teens.
- Content and context may matter.
- Effects of exposure before 3 largely unexamined at the time.

## Limitations

- Narrative synthesis, no pooled estimate.
- Mostly cross-sectional inputs.
- A co-author (Heffler) is the main proponent of a screen-exposure model of autism.

## What it does NOT show

- Which came first.
