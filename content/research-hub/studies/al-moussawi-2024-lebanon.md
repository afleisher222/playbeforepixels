## In plain language

Researchers in South Lebanon surveyed 73 children aged 0 to 3 (50 boys, 23 girls) using a behavior questionnaire drawn from the literature and the Childhood Autism Rating Scale (CARS). Children whose screen exposure began at 0 to 6 months had the highest average scores. The authors interpret this as a greater "predisposition to virtual autism".

## Who was studied

73 children aged 0 to 3, Tyre province, Lebanon.

## What was found

- Highest average behavior and CARS scores among children first exposed at 0 to 6 months.

## Limitations

- Small convenience sample; cross-sectional; parent-reported exposure.
- The questionnaire was partly built by the authors.
- No diagnostic assessment.
- Uses the term as if it were an established condition.

## What it does NOT show

- Which came first, or that screens contributed.
