## In plain language

This French paper develops the EPEE proposal: the same set of signs in heavily screen-exposed young children, and the same claim that they lessen once exposure stops.

## Who was studied

Young children seen in clinical practice in France.

## What was found

- Restates and develops the EPEE description from clinical experience.

## Limitations

- Clinical observations without comparison groups or blinded assessment.
- EPEE is not recognized by the WHO (ICD) or the DSM.
- The author list is unconfirmed.

## What it does NOT show

- That EPEE is a distinct condition, or that reducing screens is the reason for any change seen.
