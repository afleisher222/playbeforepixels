## In plain language

Researchers in Saudi Arabia surveyed parents of children aged 4 to 6 using an Arabic version of the Social Communication Questionnaire. About 23% of children had under 1 hour a day of screens, 31% had 1 to 2 hours, and 47% had more than 2 hours. One of our research passes found a summary saying more screen time went with higher questionnaire scores. The other found no summary showing the result and removed that claim. Until the paper is read, we do not say what it found.

## Who was studied

Children aged 4 to 6 in Saudi Arabia (number [VERIFY]).

## What was found

- Screen exposure: 22.6% under 1 h/day, 30.7% 1-2 h/day, 46.7% over 2 h/day.
- The association result is unconfirmed.

## Limitations

- Cross-sectional, parent-reported, using a screening questionnaire rather than a diagnosis.
- Result unconfirmed.

## What it does NOT show

- The direction of any association, or a cause.
