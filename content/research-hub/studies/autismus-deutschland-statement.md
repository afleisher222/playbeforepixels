## In plain language

Germany's national autism association says "virtueller Autismus" is not a recognized medical term but a popular or media expression. It warns that linking autism to screens gives the false impression that autism results from how parents behave, a step back to the parent-blaming myths of the second half of the 20th century. It also acknowledges that excessive media use does affect early childhood development.

## Who was studied

Not a study. An organizational statement.

## What was found

- The term is not a recognized medical term.
- Linking autism to screens risks reviving parent blame.
- Heavy media use does affect early development, which the association acknowledges.
