## In plain language

This meta-analysis pooled 10 studies with 197,357 children, 4,599 of them autistic. Autistic children had more screen exposure than controls. The authors call for prospective studies to check the relationship.

## Who was studied

10 studies, 197,357 children (4,599 autistic).

## What was found

- Pooled weighted mean difference 0.27 (95% CI 0.12-0.41), units [VERIFY], probably hours a day.
- Pooled odds ratio [VERIFY].

## Limitations

- Only 10 studies, mostly cross-sectional or case-control.
- More screen time among autistic children fits equally well with autistic children being drawn to screens.
- Handling of publication bias and heterogeneity [VERIFY].

## What it does NOT show

- That screens contribute to autism.
