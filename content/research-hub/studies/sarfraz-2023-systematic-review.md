## In plain language

This review searched several databases for studies from the preceding six years. It found mixed results. More early screen time went with poorer language, while better-quality exposure went with better language. A search snippet attributes to it the conclusion that there are still not enough data to confirm whether any association exists between early screen time and autism or autism-like signs [VERIFY].
