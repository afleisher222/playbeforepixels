## In plain language

Clinicians in Nepal argue that the presentation called "virtual autism" is neither virtual nor autism. They say it can resemble autism without meeting diagnostic criteria, and that the label adds stress and stigma for families of children newly diagnosed as autistic. They propose "screen-induced developmental deviation" as a research term.

## Who was studied

Not a study.

## What was found

- The label adds stigma; the presentation should not be called autism.

## Limitations

- A viewpoint only.
- The proposed replacement says "screen-induced", which assumes a causal direction that the evidence has not established.

## What it does NOT show

- It is commentary, so it neither shows nor rules out any effect of screens.
