## In plain language

Two Indian psychiatrists trace the term to Zamfir (2018) and Harlé (2019) and review the case reports and associations. They conclude that the evidence is too preliminary for "virtual autism" to be recognized as a diagnosis or included in classification systems. They stress that the relationship may run both ways: screens may crowd out social learning, and children with early autistic traits may be drawn to screens.

## Who was studied

No new data.

## What was found

- The evidence is too preliminary for a diagnostic category.
- The link may run in both directions.
