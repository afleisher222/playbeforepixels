## In plain language

In 2017 a French maternal and child health physician's warnings, including a widely shared video, set off public alarm in France that heavy tablet and smartphone use could produce an autism-like picture in toddlers. This handout, hosted by a French outpatient-paediatrics association, is part of that episode.

## Who was studied

Observations of children under 4 with heavy tablet and smartphone exposure in the Paris region [VERIFY].

## What was found

- Presents clinical observations suggesting a link. The contents could not be confirmed.

## Limitations

- Anecdotal clinical observation, not peer reviewed.
- French scientific and autism-community commentators called the claim subjective and without scientific foundation [VERIFY]. See our page on the psychologiescientifique.org critique.
- Authorship and contents are unconfirmed.

## What it does NOT show

- Anything about how common, how lasting or how screen-related the children's difficulties were.
