## In plain language

A Turkish review presents "sanal otizm" (virtual autism), credited to Zamfir, as a developmental risk. It argues that long screen exposure before age 3 may delay language, social communication, attention and self-regulation, and recommends limiting early screens and strengthening parent-child interaction.

## Who was studied

No new data.

## What was found

- Recommends fewer early screens and more parent-child interaction.

## Limitations

- A non-systematic review.
- Publication details unconfirmed.

## What it does NOT show

- That screens produce autism-like signs.
