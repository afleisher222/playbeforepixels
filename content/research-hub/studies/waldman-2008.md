## In plain language

Economists compared county-level autism rates among school-age children with county rainfall in three US states. Rainier counties had higher autism rates. The authors speculated that more indoor time before age 3, including TV, might act as a trigger. This is an early origin of the "TV and autism" idea.

## Who was studied

Counties in California, Oregon and Washington; children born 1987-1999.

## What was found

- County rainfall correlated with county autism prevalence.

## Limitations

- No information about any individual child's screen use.
- County-level patterns cannot be assumed to apply to individuals (the ecological fallacy). A published critique highlighted this cross-level bias.
- A later analysis tied the rainfall pattern to other county features [VERIFY].

## What it does NOT show

- Anything about TV and any individual child.
