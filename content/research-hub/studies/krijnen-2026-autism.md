## In plain language

Autism researchers at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, including an autistic co-author, argue that using the word "autism" for screen-associated developmental difficulties raises conceptual and ethical problems. They say it reinforces outdated ideas of parental blame, misrepresents autism as it is understood today, and may lead the public to believe that screen use produces autism. They call for precise, responsible language and suggest an alternative such as "screen-related developmental delay".

## What was found
