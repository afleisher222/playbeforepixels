## In plain language

Using the 2011-2012 US National Survey of Children's Health, this study compared parent-reported daily media use in 1,393 autistic children and 64,163 non-autistic children aged 6 to 17. Total screen time was similar (3.21 vs. 3.46 hours a day; not a significant difference).

## Who was studied

65,556 US children aged 6 to 17.

## What was found

- No significant difference in total screen time.

## Limitations

- School-age children, not early exposure.
- Parent report; cross-sectional.
- It partly contrasts with reviews that find more use among autistic children.

## What it does NOT show

- Anything about screens in the first years.
