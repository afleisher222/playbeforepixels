## In plain language

This Romanian psychology site argues that the only "study" behind "virtual autism" is an essay that lacks scientific rigor and was not published in a recognized scientific journal. It says that recognizing a new disorder would require clear scientific evidence and peer review.

## Who was studied

Not a study. A response to Zamfir's term.

## What was found

- Argues that the evidence behind the term does not meet the standard for recognizing a new condition.
