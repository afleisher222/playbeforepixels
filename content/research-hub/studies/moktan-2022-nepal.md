## In plain language

Child psychiatrists at a Kathmandu children's hospital describe more children presenting with autism-like signs alongside increased screen time during COVID-19 stay-at-home periods. They propose that screens, less social learning and less parent-child interaction may all play a part.

## Who was studied

Children seen at a child and adolescent psychiatry unit in Kathmandu during the pandemic.

## What was found

- A clinical impression of more presentations during lockdown.

## Limitations

- Lockdown brought many simultaneous changes: isolation, fewer services, family stress.
- No comparison group.
- The proposed mechanism is speculative. Only the first author is confirmed.

## What it does NOT show

- That screens, rather than any other lockdown change, were involved.
