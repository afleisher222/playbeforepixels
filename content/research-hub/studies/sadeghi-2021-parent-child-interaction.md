## In plain language

Iranian researchers ran a parent-child interaction program together with screen reduction for young children with autistic traits and heavy screen use. They reported lower autism-trait scores and changes in EEG (brainwave) measures afterwards.

## Who was studied

Young children with autistic traits and heavy screen time, Iran. Sample size [VERIFY].

## What was found

- Lower trait scores and EEG changes after the program (design and comparison group [VERIFY]).

## Limitations

- Small sample; design and control conditions unconfirmed.
- More parent-child interaction and less screen time happened together, so their effects cannot be separated.
- Outcome raters were probably not blinded [VERIFY].

## What it does NOT show

- That reducing screens, on its own, changes autistic traits.
