## In plain language

This review pooled studies of screen time in children and youth with disabilities. The largest group of studies (59) was about autistic children and youth, who had the highest reported screen time (0.5 to 7.27 hours a day across studies), followed by children with ADHD. Children aged 0 to 4 generally had less than children 5 and older.

## Who was studied

Studies of children and youth with disabilities.

## What was found

- Highest reported screen time among autistic children and youth, with a very wide range between studies.

## Limitations

- Descriptive only.

## What it does NOT show

- Why autistic children use screens more: preference, coping, family circumstances or anything else. It supports neither direction of cause.
