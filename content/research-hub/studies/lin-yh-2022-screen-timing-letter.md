## In plain language

Researchers in Taiwan responded to Kushima 2022. They argued that when screens are used, especially before sleep and possibly through effects on sleep, may matter more than total screen time, and suggested analysing pre-sleep use. The Kushima authors replied.

## Who was studied

Not a study.

## What was found

- Proposes screen timing and sleep as factors to study.

## Limitations

- A hypothesis with no new data.

## What it does NOT show

- It is a letter, so it neither shows nor rules out any effect.
