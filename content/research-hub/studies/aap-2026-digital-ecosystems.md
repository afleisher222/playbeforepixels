## In plain language

Released in January 2026, this AAP policy statement frames children's media as "digital ecosystems" of platforms, apps, games, video and AI tools, including designs built to keep users engaged. A summary citing the AAP's parent website says it and its technical report replace earlier AAP guidance on media. Summaries say it no longer leads with a single daily hour limit and focuses on what a child uses and what it crowds out, and that infants under 18 months learn best from real-world interaction. Whether the 2016 advice for under-18-month-olds and 2-to-5-year-olds was kept word for word is not confirmed.
