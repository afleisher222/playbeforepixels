## In plain language

Using the Longitudinal Study of Australian Children, researchers followed autistic children, children with ADHD and children with neither. More early screen time went with a small but significant steeper rise in emotional-problem scores in all groups. Screen time did not worsen trajectories more for autistic children or children with ADHD than for others.

## Who was studied

Children in the Longitudinal Study of Australian Children with autism, ADHD or neither. Sample size and exposure age [VERIFY].

## What was found

- A small association between early screen time and rising emotional-problem scores in every group.
- No sign of a larger effect in autistic children or children with ADHD.

## Limitations

- Observational; parent-reported outcomes.
- About how traits change in children already diagnosed, not about who becomes autistic.

## What it does NOT show

- Anything about whether screens contribute to autism.
