# Research hub translation plan

**Internal. Not for publication.**

## The one rule that comes first

**Nothing is translated until the English page it comes from has passed review** (source check against the originals, autistic sensitivity read, medical-claims and legal review). As of 2026-09-28 no English page has passed: every source is secondary-only. Translating now would multiply unverified text in five languages. Translation starts page by page as each English page is cleared.

## Which pages first

Translate in this order, each only after its English page is cleared:

1. **Pillar guide** (`index.md`). The page people land on. Includes the safe framing sentence, the not-medical-advice box and the "why a link is not proof of cause" section.
2. **Early support page** (`early-intervention.md`). **Rewritten, not translated.** The US section is translated for Spanish only. For every other language, a local-services section is written fresh from that country's official sources, with each fact checked and logged. Never copy US law into another country's page.
3. **Questions for your doctor** (`pediatrician-questions.md`). Short, printable, high value. Adapt the "who to ask" line to each country (pediatrician, GP, health visitor, médecin traitant, Kinderarzt, medic de familie, pediatra).
4. **FAQ** (`faq.md`). Adapt the questions on early intervention and guidelines to each country.
5. **Glossary** (`glossary.md`). Build it together with the translation term list below, because the other pages depend on it.
6. **Study pages**, starting with the most-cited and the most important critical or null ones: Heffler 2020, Kushima 2022, Ophir 2023, Lin 2025, Takahashi N 2023, Harlé 2019, Zamfir 2018, Melchior 2022, Cai 2025, Krijnen 2026. Then the pages that matter locally: EPEE and the French critique (French); Zamfir essays and psihologia.ro (Romanian); Spitzer and autismus Deutschland (German).
7. **Library** (`library.md`). Translate the headings, the "how to read" section and the one-line findings. Keep citations in their original language.
8. **Editorial policy** (`editorial-policy.md`). Needs a legal check in each target market.

`translation-plan.md` itself is never translated or published.

## Review rule (binding for every translated page)

1. **Human translation only for publication.** Machine translation may be used for a first draft, but a qualified human translator who is a native speaker of the target language, with health-literacy experience, produces the text that is published. Disclose AI assistance where the channel's rules require it.
2. **Second bilingual reviewer.** A second native speaker checks the translation against the English for meaning, especially every hedge ("associated with", "not a diagnosis", "not proof of cause"). A lost hedge is a failed review.
3. **Autistic sensitivity reader in that language community**, paid, before first publication of each page type. Terminology follows that community's own preferences [VERIFY per language: e.g. French "personne autiste"; German "autistische Person" / "Autist*in"; Spanish "persona autista"; Portuguese "pessoa autista"; Romanian "persoană autistă"].
4. **Locked safe framing sentence.** The safe framing sentence, the short version and the closing line are translated once, approved through steps 1-3 plus medical-claims review, and then reused word for word. No page paraphrases them.
5. **Local facts are sourced locally.** Any statement about services, rights, guidelines or professional bodies in a country is checked against that country's official source and logged in ops/RESEARCH-LOG.md. [VERIFY] marks stay until then.
6. **Citations stay as published.** Study titles and journal names are never translated in the citation itself; a translated title may follow in brackets. Where a source was published in the target language (French EPEE papers, Romanian essays, German Spitzer and autismus Deutschland), link the original.
7. **Same compliance gate.** Each translated page passes the full compliance gate: no product links, one printable link framed as play, no autism keywords on any product or ad in that language, no named company or school.
8. **Stay in sync.** Each translated page records the English version and `last_reviewed` date it was made from. When the English page changes in substance, the translation is marked "update pending" within [2] weekly routine cycles and re-reviewed. A translation never shows newer claims than its English source, or claims the English page has withdrawn.
9. **hreflang and canonical tags** link each translation to its English page. Untranslated study pages link to the English version with a note in the reader's language.
