Tired days count too

Low-energy play is still play. Every play in this program has a two-minute version for days like that.

Some days you won’t have the energy for a fort or a kitchen project. Maybe you’re unwell, didn’t sleep, or had a hard day at work. That doesn’t mean the day is lost.

Look at any play in this program and you’ll find a “tired grown-up” version: two minutes, no setup, often lying down. Play doesn’t need to be big to count. Lying on the floor while your child climbs on you is play. Reading one book with the lights low is play. Humming while your child builds is play.

Here are a few favorite lying-down plays: “doctor” (your child checks your knees with a spoon), “bridge” (you’re the bridge and the cars drive under), “sleeping lion” (you pretend to sleep, they sneak up, you wake with a roar).

Also, notice this: you’ve reached the end of Week 2. Fill in your check-in and look for what helped most.

Resting is part of a good plan. So is using your screen spot as planned. The goal is a rhythm you can keep for years, not a perfect month.

Today’s play: Sleeping lion

From 18 months · No prep · No mess · Quick play
You need: a rug or cushions

Lie down on the floor and pretend to be a sleeping lion. Your child tiptoes close. Wait… wait… then wake up with a gentle roar and a tickle. Swap: now your child is the lion.

Talk while you play · Pause and wait
“Shhh… the lion is sleeping… (wait) … ROAR!”

Make it easier: Pretend to snore and let your toddler wake you with a pat.
Make it harder: Big kids play “statues”: you open your eyes and anyone moving has to freeze.
Tired-grown-up version (2 minutes): Just lie there and snore. That’s the whole play.

Safety: Play on a soft rug away from furniture edges. Gentle tickles only, and stop when your child says stop.

Plain words for: When you’re too tired to play (words for your child)

“I’m very tired today. Let’s do a lying-down play.”

“You be the doctor. Check my knees!”

Honesty about your energy, plus an invitation. Children usually love a grown-up who lies on the floor.
