# Welcome to 30 Days of Back-and-Forth

We made this program for ordinary days: the rushed mornings, the long hour before dinner and the moment the show ends. You don’t need a new routine, special toys or a perfect week. You need a few minutes, a basket of things you already have, and a willingness to wait for an answer. Every day brings one idea, one play and a few words to try. Take what fits your family and leave the rest. Some days will go sideways. That’s normal, and tomorrow’s lesson will be waiting.

Here’s how the next 30 days work. Every day you’ll get one short lesson (about three minutes to read), one easy play and a few plain words for a tricky moment. Nothing to watch, nothing to join, no perfect days required.
