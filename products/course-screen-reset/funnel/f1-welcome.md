## Today’s play: Say-what-you-see walk

*From 12 months · No prep · No mess · Quick play · You need: Nothing but you*

Walk slowly through your home or down the street together. Stop wherever your child looks and put it into words: the door, the dog, the big truck. Wait a moment so your child can point, sound or talk back.

**Talk while you play (Say what you see):** “You’re looking at the truck. Big truck. Beep, beep!”

- **Make it easier:** Carry your child and stop at just three things.
- **Make it harder:** Big kids lead the walk and describe things you have to guess: “It’s round and it ticks.”
- **Tired-grown-up version (2 minutes):** Sit by a window for two minutes and name what goes by.
- **Safety:** Hold hands near roads and driveways. Keep little fingers away from outlets and anything hot.

## Plain words for: When your child asks for a screen right now

> “You want the tablet. It’s not tablet time right now.”
>
> “Let’s look out the window and see who’s outside.”
