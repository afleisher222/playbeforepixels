Shhh… the tablet is sleeping.
So what shall we do today?

Ada’s tablet is taking a day off — nightcap and all. So Ada builds a tower (CRASH!), splashes every puddle, blasts off in a cardboard-box rocket, flips pancakes with Papa and finds the perfect library book.

A warm, funny read-aloud about a day full of play, and a tablet that simply needed a good rest. Includes “Talk about it” questions for grown-ups.
