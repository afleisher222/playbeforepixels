Talk-AlongBath Book

Duck!Bubbles!All done!

6 bath-time words to say and play

0–2 years

splash

Grown-up tip Say it as you do it. Pat the water: “splash, splash!” Then stop and wait for your little one’s turn.

pour

Grown-up tip Pause before you pour. Hold the cup up and wait. Then say “pour!” as the water falls.

bubbles

Grown-up tip Offer a choice. “Big bubble or little bubble?” A look or a reach is an answer.

duck

Grown-up tip Copy their sounds. A quack, a squeal, a splash: copy it back, then wait for more.

wash

Grown-up tip Name as you go. “Wash your arm. Wash your toes.” One body part, one word at a time.

all done

Grown-up tip Sign it and say it. Open hands, twist them: “all done!” Then wrap up for a cuddle.

Talk and play at bath time

Six words for the tub, one on every page, each with a short grown-up tip. Say it, pause, and wait for a turn. A look, a sound or a splash counts.

Talk, sign, sing and read in the language you know best.

Bath safety: stay within arm’s reach every second. This book is for talk and play, not a float or a seat.

Care: squeeze out water, wipe dry and stand the book open to dry after each bath. Check every page before each bath; stop using it if anything splits or peels.
