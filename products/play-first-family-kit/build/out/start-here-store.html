Play-First Family Kit

Thank you! Here’s what each file holds and how to print and fill it in. About 20 minutes to prep, then reusable.

Your files
  • play-first-family-kit.pdf · Full color, US Letter
  • play-first-family-kit-a4.pdf · Full color, A4
  • play-first-family-kit-low-ink.pdf · White backgrounds, line art to color, US Letter
  • play-first-family-kit-low-ink-a4.pdf · Low-ink, A4
  • Canva-ready PNGs · Blank checklists, charts and plan pages (US Letter and A4 sizes) to decorate in Canva or any photo app

Pick one file for your paper size. Color and low-ink files hold the same tools; the color file adds 4 colorways of each checklist.

Printing
  • Print at 100% / Actual size.
  • Print only the pages you need. Page 2 of each file is a contents list.
  • Cardstock for tokens, cards and the board.
Filling in
  • Open the PDF in free Adobe Acrobat Reader (computer or phone) and tap a line to type.
  • You can type names, dates, jobs, rules, token ideas and plan answers. Colors and pictures can’t be changed.
  • Save, then print. Or print blank and write by hand.
Downloading: use a browser, not the app

Open your download link from the order email in a web browser. On a phone, save each PDF to Files, then open it in Adobe Acrobat Reader.
