Your first week

  1. Under 1: open 100 Screen-Free Plays at the first stage and the 0–1 Play & Talk cards.
  2. Ages 1–2: add one routine chart and the busy book’s 1–2 pages.
  3. Ages 2–5: add the bored-card jar and the family kit checklist.
  4. Every week: one Play & Talk card on the fridge.

Good to know

Every set has its own START HERE page, grown-up guide and safety page. Every play follows our published safety rules, and a grown-up is always there. Most children love 2–3 plays from each set: that’s the collection working.
