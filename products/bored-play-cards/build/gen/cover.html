Cook from a Recipe

You need

A simple recipe and its ingredients

Try it

Your child reads the recipe, gathers everything and cooks with you: pancakes, pasta salad, smoothies.

Talk

“What step was trickiest?”

Ice Rescue

You need

Big toys frozen in a tub of ice, warm water, spoons

Try it

Freeze big toys in a tub overnight. Rescue them with warm water and spoons.

Talk

“What melts fastest? Why?”

Tower and Crash

You need

Big blocks or empty cereal boxes

Try it

Stack them up together and count “one, two, three.” Then your child knocks it down. Again!

Talk

“Up, up, up… crash!”
