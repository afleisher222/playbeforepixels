Games & Puzzles

Ice Rescue

You need

Big toys frozen in a tub of ice, warm water, spoons

Try it

Freeze big toys in a tub overnight. Rescue them with warm water and spoons.

Talk

“What melts fastest? Why?”

Water play: grown-up within arm’s reach the whole time.Summer · 02

Tower and Crash

You need

Big blocks or empty cereal boxes

Try it

Stack them up together and count “one, two, three.” Then your child knocks it down. Again!

Talk

“Up, up, up… crash!”

Grown-up within reach. Every item bigger than a toilet-paper tube.1–3 · 04
