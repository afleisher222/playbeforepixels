Soft!Bumpy!Crinkle!

6 first words to touch, look and say

0–1 year

hi!

Grown-up tip Say it close up, then wait. A look, a wiggle or a coo is baby’s turn. Say “hi!” back.

look!

Grown-up tip Look together. “Who’s that? That’s you!” Pause, then copy any sound baby makes.

crinkle

Grown-up tip Crinkle, then pause. Scrunch the page, say “crinkle!”, then stop. Wait for a reach for more.

soft

Grown-up tip Say what you feel. Stroke the patch: “soft, soft.” Then bring baby’s hand to touch it too.

bumpy

Grown-up tip Name it as you touch. “Bump, bump, bumpy!” Tap the bumps with baby’s fingers or toes.

bye-bye

Grown-up tip Wave and wait. “Bye-bye, book!” Wave slowly. A look or a kick is a wave back.

Touch, look and talk

Six soft pages to crinkle, stroke, tap and look into, each with one word and a short grown-up tip. Say it, pause, and wait. A look, a coo or a kick is a turn.

Talk, sign, sing and read in the language you know best.

Before each play: check the seams, ribbon tabs and mirror. Stop using the book if anything is loose, torn or worn. Play together with a grown-up close by.

Care: surface wash with a damp cloth, or hand wash cold and air dry flat (confirm with the factory’s fabric test). Do not tumble dry or iron the mirror.
