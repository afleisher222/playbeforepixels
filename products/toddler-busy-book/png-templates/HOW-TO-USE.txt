HOW TO USE IN A DESIGN APP
1. Create a custom-size design at the size in the file name.
2. Upload or place the PNG so it fills the design exactly.
3. Add your own pictures and words on top. Keep text at least 0.15 in from the edges.
4. Export as a print-quality PDF and print at 100% (actual size) on cardstock.

SAFETY
Pieces you make follow the same rules as the book: keep every piece at least 2 in (5.1 cm)
on its shortest side, and 2.5 in (6.4 cm) for 1-2 years (use the big 3 x 2.5 in piece card),
a grown-up keeps the pieces, no hook-and-loop dots for children under 3, no balloons, beads, buttons, coins or strings, and always play together.
