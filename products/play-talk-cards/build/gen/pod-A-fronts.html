How to play

  1. Find your child’s color.
  2. Pick a card, or let your child pick.
  3. Play while it’s fun. Stop before it isn’t.
  4. Try the talk tip. Then wait.
  • 0–1 year
  • 1–2 years
  • 2–3 years
  • 3–5 years

Every card: a grown-up plays along and stays within reach.

0–1 yr 01

Peekaboo Cloth

Needs A dish towel

From 4 moPrep 0 minNo mess~5 min

Hide your face behind the towel, then pop out: “Peekaboo!” Later, let baby pull it off your head.

Talk tip · Pause and wait

Pause before “boo.” A kick, a grin or a squeal is baby’s turn.

With a grown-up. Put the towel away after play; never leave it in the crib.

0–1 yr 02

Copy Cat Faces

Needs Just you

From birthPrep 0 minNo mess~3 min

Hold baby face to face, close enough to see you well. Open wide, smile big, stick out your tongue. Watch baby try it too.

Talk tip · Repeat and add one

Copy baby’s sounds back: “ba” becomes “ba-ba!” Then wait for another.

With a grown-up. Stay within reach. Every item bigger than a toilet-paper tube.

Needs A pot and a wooden spoon

From 6 moPrep 1 minNo mess~5 min

Talk tip · Say what you see

Name the sounds as they happen: “Bang! Bang! Soft… LOUD!”

With a grown-up. Stay within reach. Every item bigger than a toilet-paper tube.
