How to play

  4. Try the talk tip. Then wait.

Peekaboo Cloth

Needs A dish towel

From 4 moPrep 0 minNo mess~5 min

Hide your face behind the towel, then pop out: “Peekaboo!” Later, let baby pull it off your head.

Talk tip · Pause and wait

Pause before “boo.” A kick, a grin or a squeal is baby’s turn.

With a grown-up. Put the towel away after play; never leave it in the crib.

1–2 yrs 20

Pillow Mountain

Needs Couch cushions

From 12 moPrep 2 minLow mess~10 min

Pile the cushions on the floor to make a mountain. Help your child climb up, then slide down.

Talk tip · Say what you see

Say it as they move: “Up, up, up! Down!”

With a grown-up. On the floor, away from furniture corners. Stay within reach.

2–3 yrs 33

Sock Puppet Chat

Needs A clean grown-up sock, a marker (yours)

From 24 moPrep 2 minNo mess~5 min

Draw eyes on the sock and slip it on your hand. The puppet says hello, asks a question, then waits.

Talk tip · Pause and wait

Puppets are patient. Give extra time to answer.

With a grown-up. Draw the face on: no buttons or stick-on eyes. You keep the marker.

3–5 yrs 44

Box Rocket

Needs A big box, crayons

From 36 moPrep 5 minLow mess~15 min

Draw buttons and windows on the box. Climb in, count down from ten, and blast off to the moon.

Talk tip · Follow their lead

Ask “Where are we flying? What’s out the window?”

With a grown-up. Pull out staples and tape first. A grown-up stays nearby.
