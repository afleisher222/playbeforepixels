Six rules for this line

  • Grown-up sizes only. No kids' tees or onesies: they need children's-product testing and a certificate (DEMAND-CHECK cut, CAMPAIGN-BIBLE [CPSIA]).
  • Print on demand only. The partner prints and ships each order. Nothing is ever stocked, packed or shipped by the founder.
  • The logo comes from brand/logo/, unaltered. No retyping, recoloring, effects or new lockups.
  • ™, never ®. PLAY BEFORE PIXELS is not registered or filed yet.
  • Only slogans ORIGINALITY.md keeps for that item (the build checks), searched before publishing, and never an event name.
  • No condition words (diagnosis or condition terms, see BRAND.md) on merch, listings, tags or ads.

Set up once, then it runs

Putting it on sale

  1. Pick one print partner (Printful, Printify or Gelato, per commerce/storefront-setup-guide.md) that offers DTG on all four colors, inside-neck label printing, and both Etsy and the site's store integration.
  2. Pick one mid-weight unisex cotton blank. Save its size chart. Fill in fiber and origin in build/build.py, then run build/render.sh.
  3. Create the tees. Logo tee: upload tee-logo_light.png to White, Natural and Mustard and tee-logo_dark.png to Navy. Slogan tee (after its search): the same with tee-more-talk-less-tap_light|dark.png. Sizes XS–3XL. Match the placement on pages 3 and 8.
  4. Add the labels, light files for light colors and dark files for Navy, one per size. If labels are not offered, remove the label panel from listing image 4 before listing.
  5. Order one sample per color. Check print, colors, label and fit, wash one five times, and photograph it on a flat surface (faceless: hands at most).
  6. Etsy: list the partner as your production partner, mark the item “Designed by” you, disclose the digital and AI tools used for the art, add the size chart, and upload the seven listing images in order (listing-01…07 for the logo tee, slogan-01…07 for the slogan tee).
  7. Own site: the same listing, plus the tote bundle and the “Next for you” links (see listing.json).
  8. Amazon Merch on Demand: apply; upload the same 4500 × 5400 files with brand “Play Before Pixels”. Approval and design-slot limits apply (UNVERIFIED: current terms).
  9. Money and tax: give the partner your resale certificate, set the shipping profile, and write the returns line (the partner replaces misprints and damage; you decide on size exchanges).

Runs without you: orders go straight to the partner; tracking and delivery emails are automatic; the FAQ in listing.json answers sizing, care, timing and returns.

Bigger sizes cost the partner more, so they cost more here. Etsy fees are roughly a 6.5% transaction fee, payment processing and a listing fee (UNVERIFIED: current rates). If a tee keeps less than $8, raise the everyday price before launch, never by “discounting” later.

Never

  • A crossed-out or “was” price
  • A permanent sale or countdown
  • “Only 3 left”: print on demand never runs out

Fine

  • A real launch week that really ends
  • Black Friday, if it truly ends
  • Bundles 10–25% under the parts

When it sells

  • Late October: list with the holiday gift bundle. Check the partner's holiday order cut-off dates (UNVERIFIED).
  • March: spring screen-free week season. Say “spring screen-free week” in plain words; never print or claim the event's name.
  • Kill rule: fewer than 5 sales in 60 days with the listing fixed: reprice once, then make it bundle-only.

Before anything ships

Checks and launch list

Done in this build

  • Tee files 4500 × 5400, transparent, 300 dpi, light + dark
  • Tote files 3600 × 3600, transparent, light + dark
  • Logo placed from brand/logo unaltered; lockup above its minimum size
  • Label and tag carry PLAY BEFORE PIXELS™ (™, not ®)
  • Adult sizes only; only slogans ORIGINALITY.md keeps; no condition or event words
  • Etsy images carry no URL or QR
  • Copyright line and version on the book and tag

The founder does

  • Fill in fiber and origin; rebuild
  • Class 25 + Etsy + Amazon + Redbubble search for both slogans; log it
  • Order one physical sample per color; wash-test one
  • Approve the sample photos and the cover image
  • Written QA pass on the listing text
  • Logo checks before public use: reverse-image search, USPTO design-code search, attorney knockout (logo guidelines, page 9)
  • Insurance in place before the first sale (protection plan)
  • Keep dated order records and label photos as trademark evidence

Printer templates change. Check every file against the chosen partner's current template and placement tool before upload.

Honest authorship

The logo art was drawn with AI-assisted tools. Answer every platform's AI question truthfully (Etsy asks), and never register AI-made art as your own work.

Your own contribution is the part you can protect: your color choices (page 4), your slogan layout and slot-3 line (page 8) and your dated edits to the logo's build numbers. Commit each change to git so it is provable.
