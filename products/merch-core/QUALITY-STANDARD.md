# Merch quality standard (founder's instruction, September 28, 2026: "good quality like Primary")

The benchmark is the *feel* of premium basics: soft cotton, true color, clean simple design, and sizing that fits. We don't copy anyone's designs. Nothing here goes on sale until a sample passes this standard.

## What we can and can't match
- **Can match:** a premium blank (the base garment), soft-feel printing, simple design, honest sizing, a printed tag-free label and careful packaging, all through print-on-demand with no inventory.
- **Can't match yet:** Primary makes its own children's clothing in its own fabrics. Children's clothing (size 12 and under) stays **held**: it needs lab testing and a Children's Product Certificate under CPSIA (legal/LEGAL-LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md rows 29–30). Our merch is **adult sizes only** until sales can pay for that.

## Blank requirements (choose the specific blank at the print partner; model names from memory, UNVERIFIED)
| Item | Minimum standard | Candidates to compare |
|---|---|---|
| Tee | 100% combed ring-spun cotton (or a cotton-rich blend for heathers), side-seamed, retail fit, pre-shrunk, tear-away or no sewn tag | Bella+Canvas 3001; Comfort Colors 1717 (heavier, garment-dyed) |
| Tote | Heavy natural cotton canvas (around 12 oz), gusset, long enough handles to go over a shoulder, reinforced stitching | The partner's heaviest canvas tote |

## Print requirements
- **Tees:** direct-to-garment (DTG) with a soft hand feel. No large solid ink blocks (they feel stiff and crack), and the design sized to the chest, not edge to edge.
- **Tote:** DTG or screen print. Embroidery only for the small Maker's Seal, never large text.
- **Colors:** only the brand palette. Check each ink against the physical sample, because screens lie.

## Sample gate (required before any listing goes live)
1. Order one sample of each item in the most popular size and color (about $20–30 each, from the launch buffer).
2. Check it by hand:
   - softness after one wash;
   - print still intact after 3 washes;
   - color matches;
   - neck label readable;
   - no scratchy tag;
   - seams straight;
   - size matches the chart.
3. Photograph the sample flat. Real photos replace the mockups in the listing images, and must show no people or children.
4. Record the pass or fail in products/merch-core/panel.md. On a fail, try the next candidate blank.

## When
Merch starts after the printables are selling (February or later, per business/GROWTH-ENGINE.md). It's the lowest-margin product, so it waits.
