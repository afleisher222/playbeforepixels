How to read this book together

  1. Go slow. One word per page. Say it, point to the picture, then pause.
  2. Wait for a turn. Count to five in your head. A look, a point or a sound is your child’s turn.
  3. Add one word. If your child says “ball,” you say “big ball.”
  4. Try the Grown-up corner. Each page has one small idea. Pick any, skip any.
  5. Follow their lead. Skip ahead, go back, stay on one page. It’s their book.

Every child talks on their own timeline. If you have questions about your child’s speech or development, your child’s doctor is a good place to start.

hi

Grown-up corner

Take turns. Wave, say “hi!”, then count to five in your head. A look, wave or sound back counts as a turn.

up

Grown-up corner

Wait for it. Hold your arms out, then pause. When they reach up, say “up!” as you lift.

down

Grown-up corner

Say it big. Crouch low and stretch it out: “dooown!” Slow, playful words are easier to copy.

go

Grown-up corner

Wait. Say “Ready, set…” then pause. Let your child fill in “go!” Any sound counts.

stop

Grown-up corner

Gesture. Hold up a flat hand as you say “stop!” A sign plus a word gives two ways to answer.

ball

Grown-up corner

Add one word. If your child says “ball,” you say “big ball” or “roll ball.” One step ahead.

uh-oh

Grown-up corner

Name the moment. Little tumbles are talk moments. Say “uh-oh!” with a big face, then wait.

more

Grown-up corner

Gesture. Pause the fun. Tap your fingertips together, say “more?” and wait before you carry on.

all done

Grown-up corner

Offer a choice. “More, or all done?” Hold up a hand for each. A point is a real answer.

shoe

Grown-up corner

Follow their lead. Grabbed a shoe? Talk about the shoe: “Your shoe. Big shoe. Shoe on!”

bye-bye

Grown-up corner

Make it a routine. Same wave, same words, every time. Words that repeat are the easiest to pick up.

book

Grown-up corner

Say what you see. Point and name what your child looks at: “duck!” Let them turn the pages.

hug

Grown-up corner

Sing it. Put “hug” in any tune you know (“hug, hug, hug!”) and squeeze on the last one.

night-night

Grown-up corner

Say it. Say “night-night” to everything: the duck, the moon, even the tablet. A calm goodnight game.

Keep the words going

These words live all through your day. Use them again and again, in the same moments.
