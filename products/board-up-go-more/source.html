How to read this book together

Say ita sound to copy
Sign ita simple hand sign
Act ita move to do together
  1. Go slow. One word per page. Say it, point to the picture, then pause.
  2. Wait for a turn. Count to five in your head. A look, a point or a sound is your child’s turn.
  3. Copy each other. Try the say it, sign it or act it idea. Then copy whatever your child does back.
  4. Pick any tip, skip any tip. It’s their book: skip ahead, go back, or stay on one page.

Every child talks on their own timeline. If you have questions about your child’s speech or development, your child’s doctor is a good place to start.

hi

Act itWave hello!

Grown-up tip Take turns. Say “hi!”, then count to five in your head. A look, wave or sound back counts as a turn.

peekaboo

Act itHide your eyes… boo!

Grown-up tip Build the pause. Hide, wait a beat, then pop out. Soon your child will fill that pause with a squeal or a sound.

up

Act itReach your arms up high

Grown-up tip Wait for it. Hold your arms out, then pause. When they reach up, say “up!” as you lift.

down

Say itLet your voice slide: dooown!

Grown-up tip Say it big. Crouch low and stretch the word out. Slow, playful words are easier to copy.

go

Say itReady, set… go!

Grown-up tip Leave a gap. Say “Ready, set…” then stop and wait. Let your child fill in “go!” Any sound counts.

stop

Act itHold up a flat hand

Grown-up tip Make it a game. Zoom a toy car and freeze on “stop!” Then let your child be the one who says it.

ball

Say itBounce, bounce, boing!

Grown-up tip Add one word. If your child says “ball,” you say “big ball” or “roll ball.” Just one step ahead.

in

Say itIn it goes… plop!

Grown-up tip Repeat it. Drop blocks in one at a time: “in… in… in!” Words heard again and again are easier to try.

open

Act itClose your hands, then pop them open

Grown-up tip Wait for a signal. Hold the box shut and wait. A look, a tap or a sound tells you what they want.

wow

Say itMake a round mouth: wooow!

Grown-up tip Say what you see. Look where your child looks and name it: “Wow! Big sun!” Short and bright beats long and busy.

uh-oh

Act itHands on cheeks: uh-oh!

Grown-up tip Name the moment. Little tumbles are talk moments. Say “uh-oh!” with a big face, then wait.

help

Sign itFist on open palm, lift up

Grown-up tip Set up a reason to ask. Put a favorite toy just out of reach and wait. A reach, point or sound is asking.

clap

Act itClap, clap, clap!

Grown-up tip Copy them. Clap back the way your child claps: fast, slow, loud or soft. Copying says “I'm listening.”

shoe

Act itStomp, stomp your feet

Grown-up tip Follow their lead. Grabbed a shoe? Talk about the shoe: “Your shoe. Big shoe. Shoe on!”

eat

Sign itFingertips to your lips

Grown-up tip Talk about now. Name what is happening at the table as it happens: “Eat. You eat. Yum!”

cup

Say itSip, sip… ahh!

Grown-up tip Keep it short. Two or three words at a time: “Cup. Your cup. Sip!” Short phrases are easy to copy.

more

Sign itTap your fingertips together

Grown-up tip Pause the fun. Stop the bubbles, ask “more?” and wait. A sign, a look or a sound means go again.

all done

Sign itShake open hands side to side

Grown-up tip Offer a choice. “More, or all done?” Hold up a hand for each. A point is a real answer.

bye-bye

Act itBlow a kiss: mwah!

Grown-up tip Make it a routine. Same wave, same words, every time. Words that repeat are the easiest to pick up.

book

Sign itPress palms together, then open

Grown-up tip Let them turn. Let your child turn the pages and choose where to stop. Name whatever they point at.

hug

Act itHug yourself tight

Grown-up tip Sing it. Put “hug” in any tune you know (“hug, hug, hug!”) and squeeze on the last one.

night-night

Say itWhisper: shhh… zzz

Grown-up tip Wind down. Say “night-night” to everything: the duck, the moon, even the tablet. A calm goodnight game.
