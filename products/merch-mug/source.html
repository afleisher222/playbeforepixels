Safety and quality

A food-contact item. The sample decides.

Food contact (UNVERIFIED until the partner’s documents are on file).
  • Use only the partner’s own ceramic mug blank that the partner states is lead-safe for food contact. Ask for its lead and cadmium test report (US FDA ceramicware leach limits, and California Proposition 65) and keep it in products/merch-mug/.
  • Proposition 65: a business with fewer than 10 employees is generally exempt from the warning duty, but the partner or a marketplace may still require a warning for California shipments. Confirm with the partner; never write “lead-free” or “non-toxic” unless their report supports it.
  • Only the partner’s dishwasher- and microwave-safe rating is repeated in the listing, word for word. No metallic, glitter or color-changing mugs (usually hand-wash and no microwave).
Grown-ups only. Sold as an adult coffee or tea mug. Never marketed as a child’s cup (children’s products need CPSIA testing, which stays held).

Sample gate (full list in QUALITY-STANDARD.md)

  1. Order one 11 oz and one 15 oz.
  2. Check print sharpness, the tomato and sky colors against the palette, and that the seal and line sit centred on each side.
  3. Run 20 dishwasher cycles and 10 short microwave runs (as the partner rates it) on one mug; the print must not fade, peel or craze.
  4. Record PASS or FAIL in panel.md. No listing goes live before PASS.
