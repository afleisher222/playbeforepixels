# Mug quality standard (same bar as products/merch-core: "good quality like Primary")

The benchmark is the *feel* of a good everyday mug: solid white ceramic, crisp color that lasts, a clean simple design and honest care words. We don't copy anyone's designs. Nothing here goes on sale until a sample passes this standard.

Everything marked UNVERIFIED comes from memory, with no partner quote or document on file yet. Check each item on setup day.

## What we can and can't match
- **Can match:** a sturdy white ceramic blank, sublimation printing that doesn't peel, a simple two-sided design, the partner's own dishwasher and microwave rating, and careful packing. All of this runs through print on demand, with no inventory.
- **Can't match yet:** our own ceramics, custom glazes or boxed gift packaging. Children's drinkware stays **held**, because it would be a children's product under CPSIA (legal/LEGAL-LAUNCH-CHECKLIST.md row 9). This mug is for adults only.

## Food-contact safety (required before listing; UNVERIFIED, not legal advice)
- **Use only the partner's own ceramic mug blank that the partner states is lead-safe for food contact.** Never a third-party or "bring your own" blank.
- Get the partner's **lead and cadmium leach test report** for that blank and keep it in this folder. It should cover the US FDA limits for ceramicware and California Proposition 65.
- **Proposition 65:** a business with fewer than 10 employees is generally exempt from the warning duty, but the partner, Etsy or a retailer may still require a warning on California shipments. Ask the partner. If a warning is needed, add it to the listing before any California sale.
- Never write "lead-free", "non-toxic", "food-safe" or "certified" unless the partner's report says so in those words. The listing repeats only the partner's own rating.

## Print requirements
- **Sublimation only.** The ink turns to gas and bonds into the mug's polymer coating, so it does not fade, peel or scratch off like a decal or a UV print.
- **Colors:** only the brand palette. Check the tomato and sky against the physical sample, because sublimation shifts reds.
- **Layout:** the seal and the slogan each sit 90° from the handle, and art stays at least 0.2 in (11 oz) or 0.25 in (15 oz) from the rims. Check this in the partner's mockup tool first.

## Sample gate (required before any listing goes live)
1. Order one 11 oz and one 15 oz from the launch buffer (about $15–20 each with shipping, UNVERIFIED).
2. Check each one by hand:
   - the print is sharp, with no ghosting, blurred edges or white specks;
   - the colors match the palette;
   - the seal and the line are centered on their faces and level;
   - the handle and body have no cracks, chips or glaze flaws;
   - it arrives well packed (box plus padding) and unbroken.
3. Run the durability test on one mug, following the partner's rating:
   - 20 dishwasher cycles (normal program);
   - 10 microwave runs of 1 minute, with water in the mug.
   The print must not fade, peel or craze, and the handle must not get hotter than the body. If it fails, the listing may not say dishwasher- or microwave-safe; try the next blank or the next partner.
4. Photograph the sample on a table, faceless: no people and no children. Real photos replace the mockups in the listing.
5. Record PASS or FAIL in products/merch-mug/panel.md, with the date, the partner, the blank and its safety documents. **No PASS, no listing.**
