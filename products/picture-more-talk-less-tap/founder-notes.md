# Talk Tower Classroom Game Kit and "More Talk, Less Tap": founder notes (optional)

The kit and the bonus story no longer print "FOUNDER: …" boxes or "ISBN / barcode" boxes (ops/TESTS/print-fixes.md, 2026-09-28). This file keeps the slots you can fill later, and where each one goes. Write in your own words: your writing is the human-authored part of the kit (brand/BRAND.md, "Human authorship"), and git keeps every version.

Every text slot below is a section of `WORDS.md`. Type under the heading, then run `node build/build-all.js`.

For every note: no children's names, no employer, no legal matters, no health claims, and nothing that suggests clinical credentials or names a school, district or program.

## 1. A note from the maker (`## founder-note`)

- **Prints:** the kit's "Inside this kit" page (p2), in every kit edition (`downloads/Talk-Tower-Classroom-Game-Kit-*.pdf`), headed "A note from the maker". Nothing prints while it is empty.
- **Length:** 2–3 sentences for teachers.
- Prompts: Why did you make this game? What do you hope a class sounds like after a week of it?
