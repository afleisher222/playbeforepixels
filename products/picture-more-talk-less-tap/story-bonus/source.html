A note for educators and families

Children get better at conversation by having lots of conversations, with grown-ups and friends who listen and talk back. Every question and answer, every joke and laugh, is a turn. More Talk, Less Tap makes those turns visible, and celebrates them.

Build your own Talk Tower. Big blocks, paper strips on a wall or sticky notes on a door all work. Keep it a celebration, not a scoreboard: no winners, no prizes, and nobody has to talk until they are ready.

Try these at circle time

  • Pause and wait. After you ask a question, count to five in your head. Quieter children, like Sam, often need that moment.
  • Say what you see. “You’re smiling! Do you have a joke?”
  • Repeat and add a little. “A pumpkin!” “A giant orange pumpkin!”
  • Follow their lead. Talk about what the child already cares about.
  • Count listening too. A follow-up question such as “What happened next?” shows real listening.

And when the tower falls? That is part of it. Build it again, one turn at a time.

Talk starters for your Talk Tower
Questions
  • What is the best smell in the world?
  • If you were tiny, where would you go?
  • What would a dog say if it could talk?
Jokes
  • What do you call a sleeping dinosaur? A dino-snore!
  • Why did the teddy bear skip dessert? It was stuffed!
Ideas
  • Let’s make up a song about lunch.
  • Let’s build a fort for the teddy bears.
  • Let’s invent a brand-new animal.
Listening
  • What happened next?
  • How did that feel?
  • Can you tell me more?
Circle-time talk games
Three quick games for ages 3–7. A grown-up leads, everyone gets a turn, and every voice counts.
1
Build a Talk Tower
Whole group · 10 minutes

You need: A basket of large blocks (or paper strips and tape)

  1. Sit in a circle with the basket in the middle.
  2. Each time someone asks a question, tells a joke or shares an idea out loud, they add a block.
  3. A good follow-up question counts too!
  4. How tall can the tower grow before tidy-up time?
2
Pass the Talking Star
Whole group · 5–10 minutes

You need: Any soft toy to be your Talking Star

  1. Give everyone the same starter, like “My favorite animal is…”
  2. Whoever holds the star talks. Everyone else listens.
  3. Pass the star to the next friend.
  4. Saying “pass” is always OK.
3
Ask Me One More
Pairs · 5 minutes

You need: Nothing at all

  1. One friend shares something: “I have a cat.”
  2. Their partner listens, then asks one more question: “What is its name?”
  3. Swap turns.
  4. Grown-ups, show it first with a child.
