Your first week

  1. Day 1: put this week’s Play & Talk card on the fridge.
  2. Day 2: print the busy book pages for your child’s band and build the binder.
  3. Day 3: cut the “I’m Bored” cards for your child’s age into a jar.
  4. Day 5: hang the family kit’s picture checklist.
  5. Any day: hand over a play coupon.

Good to know
