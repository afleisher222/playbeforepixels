What’s inside

Twenty-two words your child really uses.

One word per page in huge rounded letters, one big picture, one sound, sign or move to copy, and one short tip for you. Tap a word to see its page.

  • Before the words How to read together, in four plain steps.
  • After the words Where the words live in your day, from breakfast to bedtime.
  • For keeps A word list to tick off and pages to add your family’s own words.

Wait for it. Hold your arms out, then pause. When they reach up, say “up!” as you lift.

Every child talks on their own timeline. This book is for reading and playing together; it isn’t a treatment or a test. Questions about your child’s development are best taken to your child’s doctor.

Shipping

The board book ships from our fulfilment warehouse in 1–2 business days; the paperback is printed to order and ships in 3–5. Tracking comes by email. We ship worldwide.

Returns & reprints

If a copy arrives damaged or printed wrong, send a photo within 30 days and we replace it free. No need to send anything back.

What’s the difference between the two editions?
Same 22 words, cues and tips. The board book is smaller (6 × 6 in) with thick board pages for little hands. The paperback is larger (8.5 × 8.5 in) with paper pages, so read it together rather than hand it over.
Do I need to know sign language?
No. The Sign it cues are a few simple hand signs, like tapping fingertips together for “more”. Each is described in plain words on the page.
What is the free bonus?
A set of printable word cards and a fridge sheet that match the book. The link arrives by email with your order confirmation. You’ll be asked for an email and, if you like, your child’s birth month; never a name.
Can I use it in a classroom or nursery?
Yes. A printed book can be read anywhere. For printables you copy for a group, see licenses.
Is it sold anywhere else?
The paperback is also listed with major online booksellers, printed to order. Links appear on this page as each listing goes live.
