About

Founded by a parent and educator.

Play Before Pixels makes board books, picture books, printables and kits for the years when children learn to talk, and the years after, when they learn to read, argue and wonder.

Every book carries a small grown-up tip, written in plain words, so reading together turns into a conversation. Our research hub, the Virtual Autism Project, gathers the studies on screens and early development in one calm place, with every source cited in full.

Play Before Pixels is a trade name of AlphaPlay LLC.

How we make things

  • Books, card decks and merch are printed to order by specialist printing partners and shipped from the facility nearest you. Nothing waits in a warehouse.
  • Printables, licenses and the written course are delivered by email the moment you pay.
  • Group kits are presented by a member of your own group. We don’t run live sessions, coaching or calls.
  • Every activity is checked against our safety rules: grown-up supervision, no small parts for under-threes, no balloons for under-eights, water play always supervised.

Questions

Do I need to stop all screen time to use your books?

No. There’s no shame here and no rules to follow. The books simply make it easier to have more talk and play in the day you already have.

Is any of this therapy?

No. Our books, printables and kits are parent education. They aren’t therapy or medical advice, and they don’t treat or prevent any condition. For questions about your child’s development, talk with your child’s doctor.

Can I talk to someone about my family’s situation?

We don’t offer coaching, consultations or calls. Everything we make is designed to be used on your own, at your own pace.

Do you ship outside the US?

Yes. Books and merch are printed by partners in several countries and shipped worldwide; downloads work everywhere, in US Letter and A4.

Where does my download go?

To the email address you use at checkout, within a few minutes. The link works for 30 days, and you can ask for a new one at any time.

Privacy

We collect what we need to deliver your order: your name, email and, for printed items, a shipping address. Payments are handled by our payment processor; we never see your full card number. We don’t sell personal information. You can ask us to see or delete your data at any time.

Terms

Our books, printables and research summaries are for education. They are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Downloads are licensed for personal and household use unless you bought a classroom or group license.

Cookies

This site stores your bag and currency choice in your own browser so they are there when you come back. Nothing is used for advertising.

Accessibility

We design to WCAG 2.2 AA: every page works with a keyboard, focus is always visible, images have text alternatives and color is never the only signal. If something gets in your way, tell us through the contact form and we’ll fix it.
