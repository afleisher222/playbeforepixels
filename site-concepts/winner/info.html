Colophon

Founded by a parent and educator.

Play Before Pixels makes talk-along books, printables and kits for families of babies to twelve-year-olds, and for the teachers and parent groups who work with them. The idea is small and old-fashioned: more talk, more touch, more play, more paper.

What we make

  • Books with a grown-up tip on every page
  • Printables you can use tonight
  • Classroom packs and host-it-yourself kits
  • A free research reading room

What we don’t do

  • Coaching, consultations or live sessions
  • Apps, or anything with a screen
  • Medical advice or health claims
  • Fake discounts or countdown timers

Play Before Pixels is a trade name of AlphaPlay LLC. We don’t publish a founder photo; the books are the point.

Help centre

Answers first.

Where are my downloads? Can I get them again?
Your download link arrives by email the moment you pay, and it works on phones and tablets as well as computers. Lost it? Use the “Resend my download” link in the order email, or send the order email address through the contact form and the link is re-sent automatically.
How should I print the printables?
Every file comes in US Letter and A4, plus an ink-saver version. Print at 100% (“actual size”). Card stock of about 65–110 lb (175–300 gsm) makes cards last; laminate the ones little hands use most. A print shop can print the full-colour files if your home printer struggles.
How long does shipping take?
Printed books and merch are made to order by our print partners: printing takes 2–5 business days, then shipping with tracking. The board book ships from our fulfilment warehouse in 1–2 business days. We ship worldwide; times and costs show at checkout before you pay.
Returns, refunds and reprints
If a printed item arrives damaged or printed wrong, send a photo within 30 days and we replace it free. Because every printed copy is made for you, we can’t take change-of-mind returns. If a download won’t open, we’ll re-send it; if it still isn’t right, you get a full refund within 30 days.
Can I pay in my own currency?
Prices can be shown in USD, GBP, EUR, CAD and AUD. Non-US amounts on the site are estimates; checkout shows the exact amount. Local taxes are added at checkout where they apply.
Can I send a gift?
Yes. Add a gift note at checkout and enter the recipient’s address for printed items. For downloads, enter their email and the files go straight to them.
Can my school pay by purchase order?
Yes. Request a written quote with the form below; the quote and invoice come by email, and the files and license certificate are released when the PO is received. See licenses.
Are your books on other stores?
Paperbacks are printed to order and listed with major online booksellers as each edition goes live. Links appear on each product page when available.

    Accessibility

    Accessibility

    This site is built to work with a keyboard and screen readers, with visible focus, text alternatives for images and colour contrast that meets WCAG 2.2 AA for text. Need a large-print or plain-text version of a printable? Ask through the contact form and we’ll send one.

    Privacy

    Privacy

    We collect only what an order or the free play plan needs: your email, delivery address for printed items, and, if you choose, your child’s birth month and year. We never ask for a child’s name, and we never sell or share your details. To see or delete your data, use the contact form and choose “Privacy request”.
