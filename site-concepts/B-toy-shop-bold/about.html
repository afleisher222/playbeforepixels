About Play Before Pixels

Founded by a parent and educator.

We make talk-along books, paper play and classroom kits for children from birth to twelve, and for the grown-ups and teachers around them. The idea is small and old: talk, touch and play first; fewer screens, more back-and-forth.

Questions

When will my printable arrive?

About a minute after checkout, by email. If it hasn’t arrived, check your spam folder, then write to us and we’ll resend it.

Do you ship outside the US?

Yes. Our print partners ship worldwide. You see the delivery estimate, and whether duties are included, before you pay.

Can I use the printables in my classroom?

Home printables are for one family. For a classroom, choose the Classroom Pack with a one-classroom or whole-school license.

Do you run workshops or coaching?

No. Our group kits are designed so that one of your own members can present them, with slides and a word-for-word script.

Is this medical advice?

No. Our books and guides are parent and teacher education. For questions about your child’s development, please talk with their doctor.

Can I pay with a purchase order?

Yes, for schools and organisations. Request a written quote and we invoice you.

Policies, in short

Shipping

Books, cards and merch are printed and shipped by our partners, worldwide. Delivery times and any duties are shown at checkout. Lost or damaged in transit? Email us and we replace or refund.

Returns & refunds

Books and card decks: 30 days in new condition. Made-to-order merch: replaced free if damaged or misprinted. Digital files: we always fix or refund a faulty file. Your local consumer rights always apply.

Privacy

We collect what we need to fulfil your order and send what you asked for. We never sell your data. Unsubscribe from email with one click.

Terms of use

Our books, printables and kits are licensed for the use described on each product. Please don’t resell or share files beyond your license.

Accessibility

We aim for WCAG 2.2 AA: keyboard access, visible focus, text alternatives and readable contrast. If something gets in your way, tell us.

Educational disclaimer

Play Before Pixels is parent and teacher education, not medical advice. Nothing we sell treats, cures or prevents any condition.
