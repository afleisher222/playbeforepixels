The Grown-up corner

A tip on every page. Pick any, skip any.

Each tip is one sentence long and printed in the book, so nobody has to look anything up mid-story.

  1. go

    Wait. Say “Ready, set…” then pause. Let your child fill in “go!” Any sound counts.

  2. ball

    Add one word. If your child says “ball,” you say “big ball” or “roll ball.” One step ahead.

  3. more

    Gesture. Pause the fun. Tap your fingertips together, say “more?” and wait before you carry on.

  4. all done

    Offer a choice. “More, or all done?” Hold up a hand for each. A point is a real answer.

  5. shoe

    Follow their lead. Grabbed a shoe? Talk about the shoe: “Your shoe. Big shoe. Shoe on!”

  6. hug

    Sing it. Put “hug” in any tune you know and squeeze on the last one.
