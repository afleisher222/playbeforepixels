# Wholesale orders SOP

**Applies to:** bookstores, gift and toy shops, education-supply catalogs, birth professionals, microschools and other buyers who **resell** our products, or include them in client gift bags.

Organizations buying for their own use follow `school-orders.md` instead.

**Source of pricing and terms:** `marketing/templates/wholesale-line-sheet.md`. All tiers there are suggestions until the founder fills in list prices and the margin check.

**Gate:** group-facing, so it waits for counsel clearance, like the line sheet.

**Owners:** R = Claude routine · F = founder · A = accountant.

## 1. Channels
| Channel | When | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Direct wholesale** by email or written PO to `wholesale@` | At launch (after the gate) | Invoiced through Shopify draft orders at wholesale price |
| **Faire** | Planned for 2027 | Faire handles retailer payment terms. Commission on retailers Faire brings; 0% on Faire Direct links we share (rates UNVERIFIED) |
| **Ingram** (books only) | Now | Bookstores and libraries order our books through Ingram at the trade discount. **Point book-only retail inquiries there first**; it needs no work from us |

## 2. Screening a new wholesale account
1. The inquiry arrives. R replies with macro 27 and the line sheet.
2. **Before the first order, collect:**
   - the business's legal name and trading name;
   - its website or storefront (a physical shop or real online store);
   - its shipping address (a business address);
   - a **resale certificate** (for tier B and C buyers), valid for the ship-to state.
3. **R checks** that the business exists (website, public listing) and that the certificate is complete, signed and matches the buyer.
4. **Red flags go to F; don't ship.** Red flags: no verifiable business, a forwarder address, a request to ship before payment on a first order, pressure to dropship to consumers under our name, or a plan to list our products on Amazon or other marketplaces. Marketplace resale is not allowed without written agreement, because it conflicts with our own listings.

## 3. Pricing and minimums
- Tiers, minimums (tier B: 6 units) and discounts come from the line sheet. **Never quote below the margin floor** (2× landed cost after POD, shipping and fees). R checks each order against the margin table in the line sheet.
- **Custom pricing, exclusives, consignment and returnable terms:** F approves each. The default is **no consignment, and no returns except damaged or misprinted items.**
- **Digital licenses are not wholesale.** Retailers can't resell PDFs. Network and site licenses are sold through `school-orders.md`.

## 4. Order to delivery
1. **Order:** the buyer emails items and quantities, or sends a PO. R creates a **Shopify draft order**:
   - the wholesale price list or line-item discounts;
   - the customer marked **tax-exempt**, with the resale certificate attached to the customer record;
   - shipping by weight or zone, or the line sheet's flat rate.
2. **Payment:**
   - **First order:** **prepaid** (the card or ACH invoice link).
   - **Repeat accounts:** net 30 once F approves the account.
   - **Nothing goes to print before payment or an approved net-30 account** (line sheet terms).
3. **Fulfillment:** R places the POD or printer order shipped **direct to the retailer's business address**. Settings:
   - packing slip with no retail prices (blind or wholesale slip, where the printer offers it `[VERIFY]`);
   - our legal name and `wholesale@` on the slip.
4. **Confirmation:** the tracking is emailed to the buyer. Expected processing and transit times are stated at order time.
5. **Damaged or misprinted items:** photo within 30 days, then replaced free (macro 8). No other returns.

## 5. Marketing rules for retailers (sent with the first order)
- They may use our product images and blurbs.
- **Never state or imply any medical, therapy or developmental benefit.** Never use "autism" in connection with our products.
- No bidding on our brand name in paid search ads. No reselling on Amazon or other marketplaces without written permission.
- Don't present themselves as us or as an official partner beyond "stockist".
- Tell us about any customer complaint about safety at once.

## 6. Books and records
- Every wholesale sale is logged on the Sales Log as channel "Wholesale (direct)" or "Faire", with the resale certificate reference. Keep them out of the Maryland taxable column only when a valid certificate is on file (A confirms the treatment).
- Faire payouts are recorded as a monthly journal entry (`finance/money-and-tax-setup.md` §5D).
- Resale certificates go in the certificate file, with their expiry date. R reminds 30 days before any expiry.

## 7. Account care
- **Quarterly:** reorder check. Send one friendly "new this season" note, with the updated line sheet, to accounts that have ordered before. This is not cold outreach; the founder approves the first version.
- **Yearly:** refresh the line sheet (prices, new SKUs, discontinued SKUs) and send it to active accounts.
- Close any account that breaks the marketing rules, by written notice (F approves).
