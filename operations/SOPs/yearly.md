## Annual reviews
- [ ] **Legal (L, F):**
  - policies re-reviewed for law changes (privacy laws, subscription and auto-renewal rules, EU consumer rules, COPPA);
  - the license terms;
  - the fundraiser agreement;
  - the school "no student data" letter;
  - trademark watch.
- [ ] **Entity (A, F):**
  - still a disregarded entity, or is an S-corp election worth it at this profit level?
  - confirm whether any beneficial-ownership (BOI) filing applies. FinCEN's March 2025 interim rule is understood to have exempted US-formed companies `[VERIFY]`;
  - confirm the Maryland sales-and-use registration still covers every activity.
- [ ] **Money (A, R):**
  - the tax-reserve % still right;
  - accountant engagement renewed;
  - QBO, Link My Books and other software plans still right-sized;
  - any subscription not used in 90 days cancelled.
- [ ] **Security (F, R):**
  - full `account-security.md` §5 annual review;
  - change the email-account password (manager-generated);
  - check the recovery codes are still readable and in both safe places;
  - remove every stale device and session.
- [ ] **Trust (R):**
  - full pass of `TRUST-CHECKLIST.md`, all P0–P3 items;
  - move items up or down as the business grows (for example, BBB, certifications, EU responsible person).
- [ ] **Products (R, F):** retire, reprice or refresh any product with no sales in 12 months. Update the copyright year on reprints and new editions.
- [ ] **Records (R, A):** archive the year's `finance/closes/`, 1099s, returns, license register, resale and exemption certificates, and signed agreements. Keep tax records at least as long as A advises (commonly 3–7 years).
