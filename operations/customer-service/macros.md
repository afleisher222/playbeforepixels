# Customer-service macros (saved replies)

**Before use:** the founder approves this file once; mark it APPROVED in `ops/APPROVALS.md`. After that, the daily routine may send these replies as written, filling in only the `{placeholders}`. Anything that doesn't fit a macro is drafted into `ops/APPROVALS.md` instead. The rules are in `response-standards.md`.

**Placeholders:**
- `{first_name}`, `{order_number}`, `{product}`, `{link}`, `{date}`, `{days}`, `{quote_number}`, `{invoice_number}`, `{license_number}`.
- `{business_address}` = the PO Box in `legal/ENTITY.md`.
- Bracketed items `[like this]` come from the policies and are filled once, at approval.

**Every reply:**
- starts with the person's first name, if known;
- ends with the sign-off below;
- is sent from the address the customer wrote to.

```
Warmly,
The Play Before Pixels team
hello@[BUSINESS DOMAIN] · [DOMAIN]
AlphaPlay LLC, d/b/a Play Before Pixels
```

### 3. The file won't open
> Hi {first_name},
>
> Sorry the file is giving you trouble! A few things usually fix it:
>
> 1. **Open it on a computer if you can.** Some phones preview PDFs without fully opening them.
> 2. **Use a free PDF reader**, such as Adobe Acrobat Reader, rather than a browser preview.
> 3. **If it came as a .zip,** unzip it first (right-click → "Extract all" on Windows, or double-click on a Mac).
>
> If it still won't open, reply and tell us what device you're using and what you see. We'll send the files another way, or refund you if we can't make it work.

### 4. Printing tips, wrong size, or cut-off edges
> Hi {first_name},
>
> Good question. Printing settings trip up almost everyone!
>
> - Your download includes both **US Letter** and **A4** versions [if included for this product]. Choose the one that matches your paper.
> - In the print window, choose **"Actual size"** or **"100%"**, not "Fit" or "Shrink".
> - For cards, heavier paper (card stock) holds up best to little hands.
>
> If a page still prints oddly, send us a photo and tell us which page it is. We'll take a look.

### 5. The PDF needs to be accessible (screen reader, large print)
> Hi {first_name},
>
> Thank you for asking. We want every family and classroom to be able to use our materials. We'll send you an accessible version of {product} (tagged for screen readers, with text descriptions of images) within [5] business days.
>
> If there's something specific that would help, such as larger print or a plain-text version, tell us and we'll do our best.
