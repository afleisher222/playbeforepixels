## About us

**Who is Play Before Pixels?**
Play Before Pixels is a small studio that makes play-first books, printables and guides for families with children from birth to age 12, and for the teachers and groups who work with them. We're a trade name of AlphaPlay LLC, a Maryland company.

**Are you doctors, therapists or speech-language pathologists?**
No. Our materials are for parent and educator learning and everyday play. They are not medical, therapy or speech-language services, and they aren't a substitute for professional advice. If you're worried about your child's development, please talk with your pediatrician. In the US, you can also contact your state's free early intervention program for children under 3.

**Where does your research come from?**
We cite published, peer-reviewed studies and the guidance of major health and education organizations. Our research page lists the full citation for each one: [LINK /research]. When studies find a link between screen time and a child outcome, we describe it as an *association*, because that's what the research shows. It doesn't show that screens cause the outcome.

**How are your products made?**
Our books, card decks and shirts are printed on demand by established printing partners and shipped straight to you. Printables are delivered instantly as PDFs. [State honestly how illustrations are made, per `ops/COMPLIANCE-GATE.md` line 8.]

**Why can't I call you?**
We're an email-first business, so every answer comes to you in writing and is easy to find later. Most questions are answered on this page. For everything else, email us. Every message gets a reply.

## Using our materials (licenses)

**Can I share a printable with a friend or colleague?**
Please share the *link* to our shop instead of the file. Each family or teacher needs their own license. Additional teacher licenses are discounted on each product page.

**What's the difference between the licenses?**
- **Personal / Family:** for your own household.
- **Single-Classroom:** one teacher, with their own students, year after year.
- **Grade-Band / Site:** every teacher in the covered grades at one school or center.
- **District:** the sites named on the license.

Full terms: [LINK /licenses].

**Can I post the files on my class page?**
With a Single-Classroom license or higher, you can post them on a password-protected class page for your own students only. Never on a public website or shared drive.

**Can a therapist, tutor or child-care provider use these?**
Yes. Choose the Single-Classroom license for one professional working with the children they directly serve. For a whole center, choose the Site license.
