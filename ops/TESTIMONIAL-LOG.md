## The rules (FTC 16 CFR Part 255 and Part 465; UK DMCC Act 2024; all UNVERIFIED — confirm with counsel)

1. **Real customers only.** Every quoted testimonial or review comes from a real buyer describing their own honest experience. Never fake, AI-written, composite or simulated. Lines from `products/*/panel.md` or `marketing/CUSTOMER-VOICE.md` panels are simulated and are **never** testimonials.
2. **Nothing bought or conditioned.** No payment, discount or gift in exchange for a review, and never conditioned on a positive review. A free review copy is allowed only if the review discloses it.
3. **No insiders.** The owner, relatives, friends and work contacts never review or give testimonials.
4. **No suppression.** Negative reviews are not hidden, removed or discouraged (except where a platform removes them under its own rules for abuse or off-topic content).
5. **Quoted accurately.** Trimmed only with an ellipsis, never edited to change the meaning. No health, development, therapy or speech claims, even if the customer made one: such a line is not used.
6. **Consent in writing** before any use outside the platform where it was posted. Attribution is first name plus initial or "a parent in [country]" only, with the customer's permission. Never a child's name, photo, school or diagnosis.
7. **Typical results.** A testimonial is not used to imply a result most buyers will not get.
8. Every public use cites the log ID in the source file (for example `<!-- T-0001 -->`), so the gate can check it.
