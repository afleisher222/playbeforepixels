The short version

  1. Nothing is live yet. The products, listings, website and marketing are built. A safety file, ops/PAUSE, keeps anything from being published until you say "go".
  2. Your part is about 4½ hours of one-time setup over the next two weeks, then roughly 10–15 minutes a week of yes/no approvals.
  3. The most urgent step takes 5 minutes: make the GitHub repository private (step 1 in section 4).
  4. You will only hear about money. One line a day, a short note each week and month, plus one line whenever something truly needs you.
  5. Launch budget: $500 at most (your decision, September 28). No ads, no paid reviewers and one domain at launch; everything else waits until sales pay for it (business/LAUNCH-BUDGET-500.md).
  6. Honest money: year one most likely loses about $3,300 before one-time legal costs. Break-even is about two orders a day, most likely reached around mid-2027.

1What the business is

Play Before Pixels sells play-and-talk products for families of young children: printables, print-on-demand paperbacks and a written, email-delivered course. It is a trade name of AlphaPlay LLC, which is the legal seller on every account, receipt and contract.

  • Who buys: parents of children aged 0–5 and gift-givers first. Ages 5–12, other countries' print editions and group buyers come later, each only when cleared.
  • Where it sells: Etsy and Gumroad for downloads (worldwide), Amazon KDP for paperbacks. Free Pinterest pins and an opt-in email list bring people in. No paid ads at launch.
  • What it never does: no coaching, calls or live events; no stock at home; no health or development claims; never names or criticizes anyone; faceless (logo and brand name only, never your face or name).
  • Who does the work: Claude builds and runs it on a schedule. You approve, sign and own.

3How it runs itself

Three scheduled "routines" start on their own in the cloud, even with your laptop closed. The heavy ones run overnight so they don't compete with your own daytime Claude use. All times Eastern.

Daily studio
2:47 a.m.

Monday to Saturday. Builds or improves one product, improves one page, queues posts. Paused until your setup steps are done.

Weekly research
Sun 3:52 a.m.

Re-ranks what to build next and re-checks facts marked unverified. Off: you switch it on after the business environment exists.

Daily check
6:38 a.m.

Store and site health, approvals, and your money update. Off until the first store opens.

Each run starts fresh, reads the repository, does its work, saves it to claude/live, and ends with your money report.

The PAUSE file. While ops/PAUSE exists, the routines research and build but publish, post, list, send and upload nothing. Claude deletes it only when all seven Gate A items hold (your lawyer's written yes, bank, insurance bound, money cap set, safeguards live, accounts and keys ready, policies reviewed) and you type "go". Anyone can re-create it at any time to stop publishing at once.

What you will see

Yesterday: $X in sales · This month so far: $Y · Profit this month (est.): $Z. Nothing needs you.

Weekly: earnings, profit, top three products, tax set aside. Monthly: revenue, profit, change on last month, and the one change that would earn most next month. Your only alarm: if no money line arrives by breakfast, open claude.ai/code/routines. Keep usage credits off (claude.ai/settings/usage) so the routines can never add metered charges.

6What Claude does from here

Before launch day

  • Done: listing fixes, compliance-gate records for every launch item, the Gate A file, and the legitimacy-check drafts (corporate records, insurance record, testimonial log).
  • Get the website ready to go live before the paperback, which prints its address.
  • Small fixes that don't block launch: merch "next" links, course emails that mention the paperback, and busy-book bonus piece sizes.

After you say "go"

  • List up to 5 Etsy products a week, and paperbacks as proofs pass.
  • Pin 3–5 times a day; send approved emails to people who opted in.
  • Check every live listing, answer routine buyer questions with saved replies.
  • Monthly books close, tax reserve, and deadlines prepared 30 days early.
  • Make more of what sells; fold what doesn't.

What Claude never does without your written APPROVED line: spend money, email or message a person, post in a group, add a platform, or change terms. It never contacts anyone by phone or in person, and never writes about you, your family or your work.

  • The range is wide. One future in ten sells more than $21,100 and earns more than $7,100. One in ten sells under $2,200. The plan's "Expected" year ($11,946) happens in about one future in four.
  • 2026 is a setup year. Most likely under $100 of sales. $1,000,000 by December is not realistic (it would take about 1,300 orders a day).
  • Each sale keeps most of its price: about 77–90% of a printable on Etsy or your own site (about 70–75% through Gumroad), about $3–$7 per paperback, about $21.37 of the $27 course.
  • Money you may need to put in: the stress test (run before the $500 decision) put the deepest point at about $7,300 if costs stay at the lowest quotes and about $10,800 if they drift up. The $500 plan spends far less at launch by skipping ads, paid reviewers, extra domains and Shopify, but monthly costs such as insurance and the PO Box continue after it.
  • What helps most: holding every cost to the lowest written quote (+$2,000), trimming about $100 a month of fixed costs (+$1,200), more traffic and better listings (+$900 each), and selling by November 1 (+$800).
  • What hurts: $150 a month of ads lowers median profit by about $720. The insurance quote is the one number most likely to move break-even (at a mid-range price, 81 orders a month instead of 63).

9The emergency stops

If you want to…Do this
Stop all publishing nowCreate a file named ops/PAUSE (on GitHub: Add file → Create new file, any text, on claude/live). Or tell any Claude session "pause". Every run checks it right before publishing.
Stop the routines completelyclaude.ai/code/routines → switch each routine off. A run already going finishes, so create ops/PAUSE too.
Cut off one platform at onceCloud environment settings → API credentials → delete that platform's key.
Stop what PAUSE can't reachScheduled posts, email automations and ads keep running on the platforms themselves. Etsy: vacation mode. Email platform: pause automations. The full list is in ops/FULL-STOP.md.
Stop any spendingFreeze the virtual card in your bank app.
Something personal is publicMake the repository private, create ops/PAUSE, and tell your lawyer.

Nothing restarts until you say so. To resume, delete ops/PAUSE (or say "go") and switch the routines back on.
