# Worldwide rollout checklist (the routine checks off one step per run)

## Region 1 — United States (launch)
- [ ] US policies live; Maryland sales tax registration covers products; CPSIA items done

## Region 2 — English-speaking markets (UK, Canada, Australia, New Zealand, Ireland)
- [ ] Local-currency pricing on the store
- [ ] KDP distribution to amazon.co.uk, .ca, .com.au (and others KDP supports)
- [ ] UK GDPR privacy wording; UK product-safety responsible person for physical goods if selling direct
- [ ] Canada: textile labelling for apparel sold direct; Australia/NZ GST via merchant of record for digital

## Region 3 — Spanish (US Hispanic, Mexico, Spain, Latin America)
- [ ] Spanish site version (reviewed) + hreflang
- [ ] Spanish editions of top printables and the play guide
- [ ] EU requirements for Spain (see Region 4)

## Region 4 — European Union (France, Germany, Italy, Netherlands, Spain, others)
- [ ] EU responsible person for physical products (GPSR) or sell physical goods only via EU marketplaces/POD that cover it
- [ ] EU VAT on digital goods via merchant of record (or OSS registration)
- [ ] GDPR consent + privacy notice; 14-day withdrawal-right wording and waiver for instant digital downloads
- [ ] French, German, Italian, Dutch site versions (reviewed)

## Region 5 — Portuguese (Brazil, Portugal), Japanese and others by demand
- [ ] Market-by-market check of tax, product-safety and privacy rules before launch
