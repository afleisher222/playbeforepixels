Account Setup Checklist

Play Before Pixels, a trade name of AlphaPlay LLC · about 2–3 hours · do the steps in this order. Everything goes in the LLC's name, never your personal accounts.

Have ready: the LLC's EIN letter · your photo ID (two IDs for the PO Box) · the Chase business card · a password manager · your phone for codes. Turn on two-factor login (authenticator app) on every account and save the recovery codes in the password manager.

1. Business bank account 30 min · online

  • A no-fee, no-minimum business checking account, with a separate savings sub-account for taxes.
  • Account holder: AlphaPlay LLC · DBA: Play Before Pixels · Tax ID: the LLC's EIN (never your Social Security number).
  • Write the account and routing numbers into your password manager; every shop below pays out here.

2. Domain and business email 15 min

  • At cloudflare.com: register playbeforepixels.com · turn on auto-renew and registrar lock.
  • Registrant: AlphaPlay LLC. Keep WHOIS privacy on.
  • Create the business email hello@playbeforepixels.com (Cloudflare Email Routing or your existing Google Workspace). Use it for every account below.

3. PO Box 10 min online + one visit

  • Reserve at usps.com (small box is enough). Bring two IDs to the counter.
  • The PO Box becomes the only public address: email footers, policies, shop profiles, KDP proof delivery.
Never enter: your home address on any public profile · your photo · your children · your employer · a personal phone number as a public contact.

4. Etsy 20 min

  • Shop name: PlayBeforePixels · Seller type: business, legal name AlphaPlay LLC, EIN.
  • Payouts: the new bank account, deposit schedule weekly.
  • Shop icon and banners: marketing/social-kit/etsy-shop-icon-500.png, etsy-banner-3360x840.png.
  • Put the shop on vacation mode. Do not list anything until your lawyer's written yes.

5. Amazon KDP + book proof 30 min

  • kdp.amazon.com · tax interview as AlphaPlay LLC with the EIN · bank for royalties.
  • Create paperback: files and text in ops/UPLOAD-PACKETS/kdp/01-100-screen-free-plays/. Author/brand: Play Before Pixels. ISBN: get a free KDP ISBN. Expanded Distribution: off.
  • Save as draft, then Order proof copy shipped to the PO Box. Don't click Publish until the proof looks right and the website is live.

6. Gumroad 15 min

  • Username: playbeforepixels · Name: Play Before Pixels · payout: the new bank account · tax info: LLC + EIN.
  • Leave products unpublished; the pages are ready in ops/UPLOAD-PACKETS/gumroad/.

7. Pinterest 10 min

  • Create a Business account · username playbeforepixels · display name Play Before Pixels.
  • Profile image: marketing/social-kit/avatar-1080.png · bio: copy from marketing/social-kit/BIOS.md (Pinterest section).
  • Claim the website playbeforepixels.com once the domain is live.

8. Hand the keys to Claude 10 min

  • Never paste a key, password or code into chat.
  • Add each platform's access key in the Claude cloud environment → Edit → API credentials (names and hosts are in ops/CLOUD-RUNBOOK.md).
  • Tell Claude "accounts are open." Claude does the listings, pins and store setup from there.
