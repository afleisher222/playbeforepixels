# Compliance gate — every public item must pass every line

Mark each item PASS / FAIL / NEEDS FOUNDER. One FAIL blocks publication.

1. No health, medical or outcome claims: no treat, cure, prevent, reverse, heal, therapy, symptoms improving, "speech took off", before/after.
2. "Autism" and "virtual autism" appear only in research-hub material, with the safe framing sentence; never on merch, in children's books or in ads.
3. Research: only allowed citations (BRAND.md) or a newly verified primary source logged in ops/RESEARCH-LOG.md; "linked with/associated with", never "causes".
4. No named or criticized school, district, company, show, creator, app, device brand or EdTech product. No local angle.
5. No mention of the founder's legal matters, employer, or children's details.
6. Outreach exclusions respected (see ROUTINE.md "Never").
7. Child safety: activities obey the small-parts, balloons, cords, water and choking-food rules; supervision stated.
8. Copyright line: "© <year> AlphaPlay LLC. Play Before Pixels is a trade name of AlphaPlay LLC." AI-generated art is never described as human-made; KDP AI questions answered truthfully.
9. Trademark/originality: names and slogans cleared in brand/ORIGINALITY.md; ™ not ® until registered.
10. FTC: affiliate links disclosed; testimonials real, released and typical; no fake scarcity; prices and subscription terms clear.
11. Privacy: no child data collected; email forms link the privacy policy; CAN-SPAM footer uses the PO Box address from legal/ENTITY.md, never a home address.
12. Faceless and self-running: nothing requires the founder on camera, live, or doing daily work; no coaching.
13. Tone: warm, no shame, no fear-selling, respectful to autistic people; readable (contrast, alt text).
14. Anything viral, press-related, legal, or touching her job, case or children → create ops/PAUSE and write to ops/APPROVALS.md.
15. Autism targeting: no product, listing, ad, keyword, hashtag or email targets autism searches or mentions autism; research-hub pages link only to the free play printable/email list, framed as play (BRAND.md "Autism searches").
