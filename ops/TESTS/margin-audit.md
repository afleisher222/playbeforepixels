# Margin audit: everything the business sells

September 28, 2026. This audit covers every sellable record in `products/*/listing*.json`: tees, tote, mug, stickers, printables, bundles, course, KDP and POD books. Two records were added by other workflows during this run and are read-only here: the bath and cloth books. The archived picture-book listing is skipped.

**Rules** (commerce/PRICING.md §1–2; COMPLIANCE-GATE 16 and 18):
- A print-on-demand sale nets at least **30% of that channel's price**. This covers merch, KDP and IngramSpark paperbacks, and POD hardcovers.
- A digital sale nets at least **$3.00**.
- Every net is at or above the record's own `price_floor`, or its by-channel, by-format or by-variant floor.
- No single printable is under $5, and KDP paperbacks are $9.99 or more.

**Method:** `ops/TESTS/margin_audit.py` uses the same loader as `check_listings.py`. It checks every channel's `net_per_unit_by_channel`, plus the tee size variants. It also recomputes each digital net from the repo's fee model and flags any that differ by more than $0.05; none did. Every fee and every POD cost is **UNVERIFIED**: no live check was possible, and no partner quote exists. Re-run the audit after every price or quote change.

## Result
**0 FAIL after one fix.** Every channel with a net meets its floor. The PENDING rows have no net yet: they are later channels (KDP activity editions, IngramSpark, Merch on Demand) or waiting for quotes (presales, POD site editions). A PENDING row is never counted as a pass. Each one must be filled and re-audited before that channel opens.

## Fixed in this run
- **Logo tee and "More talk, less tap" tee, 3XL on Etsy.** At $31, the 3XL netted about **$8.65 on Etsy, under its own 30% floor of $9.30** (27.9%). The record's single `price_floor` ($8.10, the XS–XL figure) hid this. The 3XL everyday price is now **$33**: the Etsy net is about $10.36 (31%) and the site net about $13.01. The change is in `products/merch-core/listing.json` (both tees), `build/book.py`, and the rebuilt `merch-core.pdf` and page previews. No sale has happened at $31, so this is a first price, not a "was" price.
