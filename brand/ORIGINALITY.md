# Originality decisions: names, slogans, design and logo

*Play Before Pixels, a trade name of AlphaPlay LLC. Brand-lead decisions as of September 28, 2026, made from the second originality review (four lanes) and the final logo run. This is a research-based brand decision, not legal advice.*

**Why this file exists.** The founder asked for two things: a unique logo, and a business that is original and doesn't copy anyone. This file records what we keep, rename or drop to get there. Task #9 applies it across the repo. **No file other than this one has been changed yet.**

**Decision rules**
- Anything rated **medium or high is renamed or dropped**. There are two exceptions, the brand name and ALPHAPLAY, and each row explains why.
- Where the review lanes disagreed, the **higher** rating stands, unless one lane showed the facts behind it were wrong.
- Every new name below reuses words we already own (Play-First, Back-and-Forth, Talk-Along, Play & Talk, Add-One, the tomato ball). That keeps the line consistent and cuts down on new, untested words.

**Evidence limits (read before relying on any rating)**
- No lane could run a live web search. The session's budget was used up (200 of 200), and the proxy blocked USPTO, Amazon, Etsy, TPT, Goodreads, logo galleries and competitor sites.
- The evidence behind these ratings is:
  - the repo's own files;
  - DNS lookups made on 2026-09-28 (NXDOMAIN means probably unregistered; a resolving domain says nothing about whether anyone uses it as a trademark);
  - public GitHub sources (Simple Icons, google/fonts, Google's logo colors);
  - background knowledge, marked UNVERIFIED.
- So **every rating is provisional, and every new name below is unsearched.** Task #18 reruns the searches, and section 4 lists what an attorney has to clear.
