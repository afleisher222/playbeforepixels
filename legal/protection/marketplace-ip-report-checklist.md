# Marketplace IP Report Checklist: where to file and what to include

*AlphaPlay LLC d/b/a Play Before Pixels. Research as of Sept 27, 2026. Platform forms change often, so open the live page before filing. Items marked **(unverified)** could not be confirmed on a current primary source.*

## Before you file anything (every platform)
- [ ] **Right claim type.** Copying of text, art, photos, video or PDF pages is **copyright**. Use of the name "Play Before Pixels" or "AlphaPlay" on similar goods is **trademark** (and needs a registration or application to be strong). An idea, activity type or common phrase is usually **not reportable**.
- [ ] **Right owner.** File as **AlphaPlay LLC** once the IP assignment and freelancer assignments are signed. The rights-owner account on each platform should be in the LLC's name.
- [ ] **Evidence pack**: dated screenshots (full page, including URL and date), the infringing URL or listing ID, the seller or shop name, our original URL, a side-by-side comparison, and any registration or application number:
  - Copyright: TX / VA / PA number, or the eCO case number
  - Trademark: PLAY BEFORE PIXELS serial or registration number; ALPHAPLAY SN 99650345 (allowed, pending, **not registered**, so never describe it as registered or use ®)
- [ ] **Good-faith check.** A knowing material misrepresentation in a DMCA notice creates liability for damages and fees (17 U.S.C. §512(f)).
- [ ] **Business contact details only**: PMB-format mailbox, business email, business phone.
- [ ] **Log it**: date, platform, URL, ticket number, outcome.

## Standard fields most forms ask for
1. Rights owner: **AlphaPlay LLC** (d/b/a Play Before Pixels); your role: **Member / authorized agent**
2. Type of IP: copyright / trademark / (patent: not applicable)
3. Our work: title, description, link to the original, registration or application number
4. Infringing item: URL(s) or listing ID(s), what was copied, and where in the listing
5. The six DMCA statements and a signature (see `dmca-takedown-notice.md`) for copyright reports
6. For trademark: mark, registration or serial number, classes, and how the use causes confusion

## Amazon (Seller Central listings and KDP books)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Set up first** | **Amazon Brand Registry** (free) once the PLAY BEFORE PIXELS serial number exists. Try the allowed ALPHAPLAY application if ALPHAPLAY appears on the products. **Eligibility is conflicting (unverified):** Amazon's page lists an active registered mark, a pending application filed through **IP Accelerator**, or a pending mark in certain accepted offices. Some blogs say any pending USPTO application works, while Seller Forum answers say direct filings must wait for registration. Check https://brandservices.amazon.com/eligibility?lang=en-US. Supplemental Register marks do not qualify. The mark must match the brand on the product and packaging. Enrollment based on a pending application can be revoked if that application fails. |
| **Where to file** | With Brand Registry: **Report a Violation**. Without it: Amazon accepts **copyright complaints without Brand Registry**, including for KDP books (form location unverified; ask the attorney how Brand Registry applies to KDP, where the brand field is often the author or publisher). |
| **Include** | ASIN(s), the infringing seller name, what was copied (cover, interior pages, card art), our ASIN or ISBN and link, and the registration or serial number |
| **Notes** | IP Accelerator law-firm rates vary (unverified). Separately: Amazon's insurance rule (COI within 30 days after exceeding $10,000 in gross proceeds in one month; G200386300) applies to Seller Central, not to reporting. |

## Teachers Pay Teachers (TPT)
| | |
|---|---|
| **Where to file** | TPT's **copyright form**, or email a DMCA-compliant notice (all six §512(c)(3) elements) to **copyright@teacherspayteachers.com** |
| **Include** | The resource URL, the pages copied, our original TPT or site URL, and the six DMCA statements |
| **What happens** | If the seller files **no counter-notice within 5 business days** of removal, TPT **refunds buyers who purchased within the past year**. If a counter-notice arrives, the rights holder has **10 days** to show legal action has been filed, or the resource is restored. Register copyrights early so you can act inside 10 days. |

## If takedowns fail
1. **Counter-notice received:** call the attorney at once (the §512(g) restore window is 10–14 business days).
2. Attorney **demand letter**.
3. **Copyright Claims Board** (https://ccb.gov/faq/): $40 plus $60 if the case proceeds. A pending application can be expedited for $50. Damages are capped at $30,000 per proceeding.
4. Federal court (requires a registration or refusal; 17 U.S.C. §411(a)).
