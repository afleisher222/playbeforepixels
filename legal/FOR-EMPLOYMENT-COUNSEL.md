# Questions for the Founder's Employment Counsel

**Purpose:** The founder works for a Maryland public school system and is starting an outside business (AlphaPlay LLC, d/b/a Play Before Pixels). The questions below are for **her own employment attorney only**. They are written as neutral questions. No research was done into her employer's policies, records or personnel, no one at the school system was contacted, and none of these questions has been answered here. Please do not act on any launch step that depends on an answer until counsel has reviewed it.

## A. Outside employment and approvals
1. Does the founder need to disclose or get approval for outside business activity, and if so, from whom, in what form, and before which step (forming or using the LLC, registering the trade name, selling, coaching, speaking)?
2. Do any ethics, financial-disclosure or conflict-of-interest rules apply to her as a public-school employee, and do they require any filing about AlphaPlay LLC?
3. Are there limits on the kinds of work (coaching, paid workshops, selling to parents or educators) she may do outside her job?

## B. Intellectual property
4. Who owns materials she creates on her own time, with her own equipment, that relate to the subject she teaches or to her professional expertise?
5. Is there any policy, contract, collective-bargaining term or signed agreement that gives the employer rights to materials created by employees, and if so, what is its scope?
6. Are there materials she created for or used in her teaching that must be kept out of the business entirely? How should she document that business materials were created independently (dates, devices, accounts)?
7. Does the ALPHAPLAY trademark application (spelling and literacy goods) raise any ownership or use question, given its subject matter?

## C. Separation of job and business
8. What separation rules should she follow: no use of work time, email, devices, networks, printers, supplies, logos or the employer's name?
9. May she mention her job title, years of experience, or "educator" status in marketing, author bios or workshop descriptions? If yes, what wording is safe?
10. May she sell to, coach or present to people she knows through work, such as colleagues, current or former students' families, or parent-teacher groups connected to her own school or district? Are there cooling-off or recusal rules?
11. May she accept paid speaking or workshop bookings from PTAs, child-care centers, libraries or teacher associations that serve families in the district where she works?
12. Are there restrictions on public advocacy or commentary about education or technology topics by employees in their private capacity?
13. Are there rules about using classroom experiences or anecdotes (fully de-identified) in books, courses or talks, beyond student-privacy laws?

## D. Timing and risk
14. Is there any current or pending matter involving the founder and her employer that affects the timing, structure or public visibility of the launch? (Counsel to advise; no details are included here.)
15. Should any launch step, such as publishing her name, filing a trademark, or taking paid bookings, wait until counsel confirms it is safe?
16. What records should she keep to show compliance?
