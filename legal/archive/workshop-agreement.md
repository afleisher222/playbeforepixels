> **Preparer notes (delete before use)**
> - **Before booking**, check whether the host is connected to the founder's employing district or its schools. Whether paid workshops for such groups are allowed is a **neutral question for her employment counsel** (see `PROTECTION-PLAN.md`, section 12). Do not book those groups until counsel answers.
> - In *BJ's Wholesale Club v. Rosen*, 435 Md. 714 (2013) (https://caselaw.findlaw.com/court/md-court-of-appeals/1651246.html), Maryland's high court enforced a parent-signed release and indemnity for a children's play area. The *Wolf v. Ford* limits still apply: no release of gross negligence, recklessness or intentional harm. Attachment A is written to that standard.
> - Send the host the GL certificate of insurance (COI) when asked, and ask for the host's COI in return.
