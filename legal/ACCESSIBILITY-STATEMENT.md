# Accessibility Statement

**Last updated:** [DATE]

Play Before Pixels (a trade name of AlphaPlay LLC) wants everyone — parents, caregivers, educators and organizations anywhere in the world — to be able to use our website and buy our products.

## Our goal
We aim for our website to conform to the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA** [or 2.2 AA]. This includes:
- text alternatives for images and illustrations;
- captions for videos and transcripts for audio and podcast episodes;
- color contrast that meets WCAG AA;
- full keyboard navigation and visible focus indicators;
- page structure with headings, labels and landmarks for screen readers;
- text that can be resized to 200% without breaking the layout;
- respect for "reduce motion" settings;
- tagged, accessible PDFs for printables where practical, and a plain-text or large-print version on request.
