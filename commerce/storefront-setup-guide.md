Prepared September 27, 2026, for the founder of Play Before Pixels (a trade name of AlphaPlay LLC, Maryland) and her accountant.

**What this is for:** getting every shop set up in the right order, with **every payment linked to one place**, so year-end taxes are a simple handoff to your accountant.

**Who does what:** you create every account and enter all identity, bank and tax details yourself. This guide never asks for passwords, and no platform approval is promised.

## Part A: Link the money first (do this before opening any shop)

These steps are what make the accountant handoff easy. They come before any storefront.

### A1. One legal name and one taxpayer ID on every platform
- Open every account in the legal name **AlphaPlay LLC**, with **Play Before Pixels** as the shop or display name (`legal/ENTITY.md`).
- Every platform that pays you runs a **tax interview** (a W-9). **Ask your accountant which name and taxpayer ID go on it, then enter that exact same answer on every platform.** The answer depends on how AlphaPlay LLC is taxed. The W-9 instructions treat a single-member LLC that is a "disregarded entity" differently from an LLC taxed as a corporation, and in some cases the owner's name and number go on the form instead of the LLC's (UNVERIFIED; confirm at [irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-9](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-9)). If the answers match everywhere, every 1099 matches your return.
- Walmart requires an EIN and does not accept an SSN (secondary source, UNVERIFIED: [goaura.com](https://goaura.com/blog/walmart-seller-application)). AlphaPlay LLC already has an EIN (`legal/ENTITY.md`).

### A2. One bank account in, one card out
- **Every payout goes to ONE business checking account** in AlphaPlay LLC's name, with the "Play Before Pixels" trade name added: Shopify Payments, KDP, IngramSpark, Etsy, TPT, TikTok Shop, Amazon Associates/Influencer, Merch on Demand, Gumroad (course and downloads), Faire and Walmart.
- **Pay every business cost from ONE business card:** POD partner invoices, printers, platform subscriptions and (later, only when sales pay for them) ads.
- If a platform can pay only through PayPal (TPT historically did; UNVERIFIED), use a PayPal **business** account that transfers into that same checking account.

### A3. Sales tax: who collects it (UNVERIFIED; confirm with the accountant and at [marylandtaxes.gov](https://www.marylandtaxes.gov/))
- **Your own direct sales** (Shopify, Payhip, most course platforms; Gumroad is merchant of record for its sales, UNVERIFIED): **you** collect and file. Maryland's rate is 6%, and it has applied to digital products (printables) since 2021. Shopify Tax only *calculates* the tax; it does not file it for you. `legal/ENTITY.md` says to add these new activities to AlphaPlay LLC's existing Maryland sales-and-use tax registration.
- **Marketplaces** remit sales tax for your sales there as "marketplace facilitators": Amazon Seller Central, Etsy, TikTok Shop, Walmart and TPT (TikTok, Walmart and TPT are UNVERIFIED).
- **Gumroad** is merchant of record (since Jan 1, 2025): it collects and remits US sales tax **and** EU/UK VAT on your sales. Tell the accountant so those sales are not taxed twice.
- **Worldwide digital sales:** printables sold on Shopify to EU/UK buyers can trigger VAT registration or OSS/IOSS filing. Routing international digital sales through Gumroad avoids that.
- **Other states:** watch for economic nexus in each state where direct sales grow. Maryland's own threshold is $100,000 or 200 transactions (UNVERIFIED).
- **Coaching services:** none. The business sells no coaching, consults or live services (brand/BRAND.md, binding), so this question does not arise.

### A4. Resale certificates
- Give your POD partner (Printful, Printify or Gelato) your Maryland sales tax registration or a multistate resale certificate. Otherwise it charges you sales tax on fulfillment orders (UNVERIFIED).
- Collect resale certificates from wholesale retailers who buy through Faire.

### A5. The monthly 15-minute routine
Make one folder per month, e.g. `Taxes/2026/2026-10/`. On the 1st to 5th of each month, download the items in the **Money map** below into it. Give the accountant **gross sales, fees, refunds and the sales tax that marketplaces or merchant-of-record platforms collected**, not just the net deposits that reach your bank.

### A6. Money map (every channel, where the money goes, what form to expect)

Federal 1099-K threshold: **$20,000 AND 200 transactions** (restored by the July 2025 federal law). Maryland may make platforms report at **$600**, so expect 1099-Ks below the federal threshold. 1099-NEC/MISC threshold: **$2,000** for payments made after Dec 31, 2025. **Royalties** on 1099-MISC keep a **$10** threshold. **Income is taxable whether or not a form arrives.** All of this is UNVERIFIED. Confirm at [irs.gov: Understanding your Form 1099-K](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/understanding-your-form-1099-k) and [irs.gov: About Form 1099-NEC](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-1099-nec).

## Part C: Channel by channel

**Children's products in general (UNVERIFIED; confirm with CPSC guidance):** items for kids 12 and under (onesies, toddler or kids' tees, a card deck sold as a kids' game) may need CPSIA third-party testing, a Children's Product Certificate (CPC) and tracking labels. Add toddler tees and onesies once you have your POD partner's compliance documents.**
