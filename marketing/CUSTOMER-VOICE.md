# Customer Voice: what buyers love and hate, and the product rules that follow

Written September 28, 2026, from the verified customer-voice research lanes: printables and activity books; board books, picture books, decks and parent kits; teachers, child-care directors and PTA/PTO leaders. It does not repeat DEMAND-CHECK.md (sales evidence) or BLIND-SPOTS.md.

## How to read the quotes (important)

| Tag | Meaning | Allowed use |
|---|---|---|
| **[S]** | Found word for word in the research agent's saved search output. These are search-engine summaries of the page, not the raw page. | Internal design input. Before any public use, open the live page in a browser and re-check the quote in context. |
| **[P]** | Paraphrase or reported speech in the summary, not a verbatim quote. | Internal only. Never put it in quotation marks publicly. |
| **[V]** | Carried over from an earlier pass and not re-checked (the source domain was blocked). | Internal only, [VERIFY] (QUEUE task #18). |

**Standing limits**
- Every competitor, brand and title named below is for **internal research only**. Never name them in public copy (BRAND rule 2).
- Never reuse another brand's testimonial wording (rule 7).
- Never quote any review that mentions a child's diagnosis (Autism searches rule). One such review was found and is excluded below.
- Survey figures (Gallup, EdWeek, TPT, DonorsChoose, CCBC) stay out of all listings until they have been re-read.
- Teacher, school, PTA and child-care products stay on hold until employment counsel answers. Nothing the founder made for or used in her own teaching may be sold.

## 1. Printables and activity books

### What buyers love
- **The child's engagement, described as minutes of calm.**
  - "My toddler has been obsessed with the color sorting and cognitive games for days, giving me actual, uninterrupted minutes to drink coffee" [S]
  - "Excellent product - keeps my little one entertained for ages!" [S]
  - "Endless hours of cutting and 500 velcro dots later this little one begging to play in her busy book!" [S]
  - Sources: https://www.etsy.com/market/busy_book_printable · https://www.etsy.com/listing/770011334/busy-book-mega-bundle-129-printable
- **Easy files and good-looking pages.**
  - "Great quality files! Very easy to download and printable quality is excellent" [S]
  - "Absolutely in love with this busy book. The graphics and colors are so beautiful." [S]
  - Same sources.
- **Easy assembly.**
  - "super easy to put together" [S]
  - "Easy to cut out, video provided clear instruction" [S]
  - "The template is forgiving - it works even if I think I've made a mistake" [S]. This one is from a paper craft for ages 3–5, not a busy book.
  - Source: https://www.etsy.com/market/kid_printables
- **Supplies already in the house.**
  - "you already have most supplies right in your own home!" [S] (https://www.amazon.com/Everything-Toddler-Activities-Book-Everything%C2%AE/dp/1593375883)
  - "material needed for most is things you will have on hand." [S] (https://www.amazon.com/Ultimate-Toddler-Activity-Guide-educational/dp/1987787471)
- **Labels for prep, time and mess.** A top activity book is praised for "outlining materials needed, level of messiness, prep time, and time it will take to complete the activity." [S] (https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/39087812-play-learn-toddler-activities-book · https://teachingmama.org/play-and-learn-toddler-activities-book/)
- **Clear ages and durations.** A reviewer appreciated "recommended ages and duration for each activity" [S] (Goodreads result set; exact page not pinned).
- **Files you can customise.** A reviewer was "so happy that a template was available for me to customize to my needs and my family's needs", "as a busy mom" [S]. Source: https://www.etsy.com/listing/1246633084/summer-screen-time-rules-checklist
- **Bored jars that just work.**
  - "Great ideas to help kids when they're bored and can't think of what to do. Excellent quality" [S]
  - From a kindergarten teacher: "They printed up wonderfully. Super fun activity to get kids up and moving" [S]
  - Source: https://www.etsy.com/nz/listing/1160927624/boredom-jar-cards-im-bored-jar-printable
- **A plain-language "why this helps" guide.** In the benchmark brand's reviews, the guide is what parents praise most: "an incredibly easy guide on the best ways to play" [S]. Sources: https://www.motherandbaby.com/reviews/education-toys/lovevery-play-kits-review/ · https://theeverymom.com/lovevery-play-kits-review/
- **Routine cards children can read.** Praise is for cards that are easy to see and easy for the child to understand [P], and for a toddler who loves seeing the week's schedule [P]. Source: https://www.etsy.com/listing/1229554412/toddler-visual-schedule-printable-daily
- **Excluded:** "a breeze to print and laminate" comes from a review that mentions a child's autism. Never quote it or echo it.

### What buyers hate
- **Cutting time.**
  - "I'm not going to lie the amount of cutting is kind of crazy, brings me back to when I taught Kindergarten, but it was worth it in the end." [S]
  - "A lot of cutting, but my kiddo loves it and it was a great addition into our school day." [S]
  - Source: https://www.etsy.com/market/busy_book_printable
  - DIY guides put cutting at about 1.5 hours for a 10-page busy book [S] (https://www.littleladoo.com/make-busy-book/).
- **Files they can't open.**
  - Etsy Help: "You can't download a digital purchase through the Etsy app at this time... sign in to Etsy on your mobile browser or computer." [S] (https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/115013328108-How-to-Download-a-Digital-Item)
  - Community thread titles: "The Etsy app doesn't allow downloading purchased files" and "Customer can't download digital purchase" [S] (https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/The-Etsy-app-doesn-t-allow-downloading-purchased-files/td-p/144885609)
  - Sellers estimate "one in every 15-25 customers is mobile only" [S] (https://www.etsysimplicity.com/what-are-the-best-file-formats-for-etsy-digital-downloads-and-other-questions-answered/)
  - Etsy limits: 20 MB per file and 5 files per listing [S] (https://snaptosize.com/etsy-20mb-file-limit · https://www.selfwage.com/how-to-upload-more-than-5-files-to-etsy/)
  - Canva links can make buyers "request access" after purchase [S] (https://community.etsy.com/t5/Technical-Issues/Canva-Template-Link-HELP/td-p/148025089/jump-to/first-unread-message)
- **Files that aren't editable.**
  - "This Item isn't editable and that's extremely unfortunate because the design is amazing" [S]
  - "You can't edit this", "definitely a waste of time" [S]. These are two separate fragments.
  - Source: https://www.etsy.com/listing/462967406/kids-chore-chart-editable-printable
- **Ink cost.**
  - "a full-page black background or heavy pattern can drain a cartridge fast" [S] (https://getcoloring.net/tutorials/how-to-print-coloring-pages-without-wasting-ink)
  - "Printing new sheets every day can quickly drain printer ink and paper supplies" [S] (https://happytoddlerguide.com/best-free-printable-toddler-activities-to-do-at-home/)
  - The market answers with low-ink versions: https://www.etsy.com/market/low_ink_printables
- **More prep than play.**
  - "a half hour to an hour prepping... and it only lasts 10 to 15 minutes anyway" [S] (source not pinned; mother.ly result set)
  - A counter-signal: parents search "low-prep", "no-prep" and "tired parents".
- **Special supplies.** Some reviewers say activities "require stuff that parents just didn't have" [P] (https://www.babywisemom.com/book-review-toddlers-busy-book/). This is contested: a Goodreads summary of the same book says the opposite [S] (https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/835822.The_Toddler_s_Busy_Book).
- **Surprise black-and-white interiors.** A reviewer would not have bought the book had they known it was black and white, and called the pages "blah" [P] (https://www.amazon.com/Ultimate-Preschool-Activity-Guide-Activities/dp/1952016371). The competitor's fix is a code to download a free colour version [S].
- **Age bands that are too wide.** Most activities fit only part of the stated range, and "the activities chosen were frankly boring them" [P] (https://app.thestorygraph.com/book_reviews/6a417f38-2acc-4a63-88cc-2b6fe119438a).
- **Missing cards in routine sets.** One set had no bedtime tooth-brushing card, so the morning card was reused [P]. Sellers fill gaps with custom requests, which our no-contact rule forbids. Source: https://www.etsy.com/in-en/listing/1599059345/kids-daily-routine-cards-visual-schedule
- **The hidden cost of velcro and laminating.**
  - "roughly 50-80 dots for a book with 10 activities" [S]; dots "can be expensive" [S]; hooks and loops "wear down over time" [S]
  - A maker who used to pay "$2 per page for hard lamination" [S] said "buying a laminator was well worth it" [S]
  - Sources: https://www.littleladoo.com/tools-supplies-to-make-busy-book/ · https://crayonsandspice.com/simple-and-versatile-diy-busy-book-for-a-toddler/
- **The backlash against AI-made books.**
  - Indicator found "over 517" apparently AI-generated children's nonfiction books, with cover errors such as "Genshis Khan" [S] (https://indicator.media/p/amazon-is-selling-hundreds-of-seemingly-ai-generated-biographies-for-children-d536)
  - Cory Doctorow: "These are garbage books that exist to suck up money from the inattentive." [S] (https://aiweekly.co/alerts/amazons-ai-generated-childrens-books-flood-gift-piles)
- **Screen time as a reward backfires.** "When screens become the payoff, homework, chores and family routines can start to feel like obstacles to the thing children want most" [S] (Stacker, syndicated August 2026: https://abc17news.com/stacker-parenting-family/2026/08/07/does-screen-time-affect-behavior-why-using-it-as-a-reward-can-backfire/). Many best-selling screen-time charts are "earn your screen time" ticket systems (https://www.etsy.com/listing/1732559168/screen-time-tickets-printable-reward).
- **Unsafe ideas in popular lists.** Lists recommend "Balloon volleyball" [S] and sorting "dry beans, buttons" [S]. CPSC recalled a busy book in 2026 over a small-parts choking hazard [S] (https://cpsc.gov/Recalls/2026/Cobelae-Busy-Books-Recalled-Due-to-Risk-of-Serious-Injury-or-Death-from-Choking-Hazard-Violates-Small-Parts-Ban-Sold-on-Amazon-by-Flyindream). Never name the product or the list authors publicly.
- **Marker bleed-through on KDP paper.** This comes from publisher guidance, not counted reviews: "single-sided printing is strongly recommended to prevent marker bleed-through" [S] (https://www.kdpeasy.com/blog/publish-coloring-book-amazon-kdp).

### Untested gap
Parents may want to hand the screen plan and play ideas to sitters and grandparents. The demand pages exist (https://www.etsy.com/market/babysitter_printable · https://barleyandbirch.com/100-zero-prep-kids-activities-for-any-time-anywhere-with-a-free-printable/), but no buyer states this wish directly. Treat it as a hypothesis.

## 2. Picture books and board books (all [V] unless noted)

### What buyers love
- **Humour over lecture.**
  - "silly, loving nose-tweak to digital civilization" (https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/matthew-cordell/hello-hello-cordell/)
  - "a truly hysterical parody" (https://www.themarginalian.org/2011/11/14/goodnight-ipad/)
  - "without ever feeling preachy or judgemental toward parents" (https://www.readingzone.com/books/screen-time/)
- **Books a parent can survive rereading.** "not torturous for a parent to read again and again" (https://theexperiencedmama.com/best-sandra-boynton-books/)
- **Routine-based first words with caregiver tips.**
  - "a great guide for parents"
  - An 18-month-old "more interactive with this book than any other"
  - Sources: https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/213197182-100-first-words-ms-rachel · https://talkwithmebook.com/
- **Seeing their own family.**
  - "People underestimate how important it is to see families like yours in children's books." (https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/32666685-baby-s-first-words)
  - Grace Lin: "There are so many books of animals and the ones with people tended to be white babies" (https://happilyeverelephants.com/home/diverse-baby-books/)
- **No shame.** "never once felt judged or shamed" (https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/203656907-the-screentime-solution)

### What buyers hate
- **Preachiness.** Readers "expected it to be preachy and didactic" (https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/58152485-polly-and-the-screen-time-overload).
- **Books that fall apart, which can become a safety issue.**
  - "the spine ripped off"; "they get torn quickly" (https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/284652.First_100_Words_Big_Board_Book)
  - "completely came apart after just a few times"
  - A child "chewed through part of it and actually got choked on a chunk that came off" (https://paperpielearning.com/ProductTab/ProductReviews/5044)
  - Lift-the-flap books break early (https://www.readbrightly.com/robust-lift-the-flap-books/).
- **Books that feel too short.** "just so short"; "a little disappointed there wasn't more".
- **No story.** First-words books with "no storyline".
- **A book the parent is tired of.** "get it from the library so you have a reason to stop reading it" (https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/205307721-ms-rachel-and-the-special-surprise).
- **Odd or dated pictures.** "a cordless landline"; "uncommon specimens of items instead of baseline forms" (First 100 Words, Goodreads).
- **Activities that don't work.**
  - "required special materials or skills they didn't have" (https://picklebums.com/150-screen-free-activities-for-kids/)
  - Instructions "too brief", "not all activities had been thought through or play-tested" (https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/36374011-screen-free-fun)
- **Print-on-demand quality.** "dull and not super crisp", "really bad paper quality", "dinged up, bent books" (https://www.kdpcommunity.com/s/question/0D52T00005F5jWaSAJ/childrens-picture-book-quality-control-is-horrible?language=en_US).
- **Guilt.** Parents feel widespread screen-time guilt. The statistics are unverified, so never publish them (https://ehe.osu.edu/news/listing/how-avoid-screen-time-guilt-advice-parents).

## 3. Conversation and play card decks (all [V])

### What buyers love
- **Cards that get past one-word answers.**
  - Cards "got their 9-year-old talking the first night"
  - A daughter who said "I don't want to talk about it" "now asks for the cards every night"
  - Sources: https://justcaracarroll.com/200-conversation-cards-for-kids/ · https://tellmecards.com/
- **Heavy stock and a good box.** A 350gsm matte deck with tabbed dividers is praised (https://bestself.co/products/little-talk-deck).

- **Repetition.** "asking them the same questions over and over again breed the same answers"

- **Cheap card stock and boxes.** The "faded ink, torn edges" story came from an auto-generated AliExpress page and was **deleted as unreliable**.

## PRODUCT RULES (numbered; every new product and every revision)

Rules marked (gate) are already in ops/COMPLIANCE-GATE.md and are restated here so the whole set sits in one place.

### Files and delivery
1. **Every printable ships a Color file and a Low-ink file, each in US Letter and A4.** Low-ink means a white background, no full-bleed tints, and line art children can colour. Brand tints appear only in the Color file and in mockups. (gate #19)
2. **Never ship a zip.** Ship plain PDFs of 15 MB or less each, with plain file names.
3. **Etsy five-file budget:** START HERE + Color Letter + Color A4 + Low-ink Letter + Low-ink A4. Fillable, editable, duplex and big-piece versions are merged into those files. On our own store they may be separate downloads.
4. **File 1 is always "START HERE.pdf":** what each file holds, print settings, and "use a browser, not the app".
   - Own-store edition: add a QR code to the bonus and re-download pages.
   - Etsy and TpT editions: **no URL or QR code** (gate #16). Point to the marketplace's Purchases page instead.
5. **The last listing image is "How to download: use a browser, not the Etsy app".** Put the same text in the automatic order message and the saved reply. On our own store, send a direct link plus an email copy, valid for at least 1 year.
6. **Canva is never the only format.** Offer it only as a bonus alongside a fillable PDF.
7. **"Version x.y · Month Year" goes in the footer of every PDF and POD interior.** Tell past buyers about every update, and never swap a file quietly. Safety fixes follow ops/SAFETY-CORRECTION.md.

### Prep, play time and supplies
8. **Show a prep budget in the listing and on page 1,** for example "About 25 min to prep, then reusable". Put a 0 / 2 / 10-min prep icon on every page.
9. **Cutting layout:**
   - Straight-line grid cutting only, so a paper trimmer can do it; no contour cutting.
   - Thick cut lines and at least 1/8 in of white margin.
   - No more than 12 cut pieces per activity page.
   - Label every cut sheet with the page it belongs to.
10. **Every build-it pack includes no-cut pages** (point-and-talk, open-ended, dry-erase) so a family can play the same day.
11. **Every play carries four icons:** materials, prep, mess, and expected play time (about 5, 10 or 20+ min).
12. **No play takes longer to prep than it takes to play.**
13. **"Nothing to buy" badge** for plays that use only common household items. At least 70% of the plays in a play collection must qualify. Put a front "Pantry list" of about 25 household items, following rule 4: no beans, buttons or small items for under-3s.
14. **Every activity has a tired-grown-up version:** 2 minutes, no setup. Play collections get a "Tired-grown-up plays" chapter of plays run from the couch or the floor.
15. **Label every play with a starting age in months** ("from 18 mo") and a "Make it easier / Make it harder" pair. The easier version must still pass the under-3 safety rule. Describe ages honestly: "Best for 2–3; easier versions for 18 mo+".
16. **Page 2 of every build-it printable is a shopping list with exact counts** (sheets of cardstock, pouches, dots), plus a no-laminator route using page protectors. The PDF links only to our own site's Setup-kit page. Amazon Associates links appear only on that web page, with the disclosure, and **never** in PDFs, PDF QR targets or emails.
17. **Design for reuse where it fits.** Mazes, tracing, matching and "draw the missing part" pages are made for dry-erase sleeves and marked "Print once, play again".

### Safety
18. **Every cut-piece page prints: "Grown-up keeps the pieces. For under-3s, use the big-piece version."** Big-piece grid cells are at least 1.5 in, larger than the toilet-paper-tube test.
19. **Velcro:**
    - Under-3 versions use no loose velcro dots. Use lay-on-top matching, pockets in a page protector, or long strips fixed to both the base and a big piece.
    - Every velcro page prints: "Check dots before each play; remove any that lift."
    - Pieces sized for 3/4 in dots are for ages 3+ versions only.
20. **Safety wording in listings: "Every play follows our published safety rules."** Never "safety-checked", "certified", "CPSC-tested" or "safe for all ages". The rules are published on an "Our safety rules" site page.
21. **Pages written for sitters or grandparents repeat the full rule-4 safety notes.**
22. **Toddler books have no flaps, tabs, pull-outs or glued parts.** Paper editions print "Paper pages: read together and keep away from mouths". Never market a paperback as a baby's own book.

### Editability and completeness
23. **Every chart, schedule, sign or label ships three ways:** pre-filled, blank write-in, and fillable-PDF fields that work in free Adobe Acrobat Reader.
    - The listing says exactly what can be edited, for example "text boxes: yes; colours and pictures: no".
    - "Editable" goes in a title only when the fields really are fillable.
    - QA includes a phone test of every fillable field.
24. **Routine and card sets are complete on the first download, because we take no custom requests.** They include:
    - every daily routine plus big-change cards;
    - two copies of high-use cards (brush teeth, shoes, wash hands, potty, snack);
    - blank "draw it" cards and a photo-frame card with a print-and-glue route, because fillable image fields are unreliable on phones;
    - a word-free version.

### Tone and content
25. **The no-guilt test** (BRAND-RESPECT-PLAN move 1).
    - Frame every product as adding talk and play.
    - Fail these words: rewiring, damage, addiction, toxic, zombie, digital drug, before it's too late.
    - Never show a parent on a phone as the villain, or a sad child next to a device.
    - The panel's "made me feel judged" and "preachy" scores must be 2 or lower (out of 5).
26. **Screens have a spot in the day.**
    - The screen slot never grows or shrinks with chores or behaviour.
    - Tokens, if used, earn play and connection only (a story, a game with a grown-up), never screen minutes.
    - Add a "What we do next" card and a 5-minute wrap-up card.
27. **Every printable and deck includes a 1–2 page Grown-up guide.** It covers:
    - setup in 2 minutes;
    - what the child is practising, in plain words with no clinical terms;
    - three talk lines (pause and wait, say what you see, repeat and add one word);
    - what to do if interest fades;
    - "Most children love 2–3 of these; that's normal."
    Show a guide page as listing image 3.
28. **Every talk-tip product says: "Talk, sing and read in the language you know best. Every language counts."** A sign, a point or a device tap counts as communicating.
29. **The story text never lectures.**
    - Teaching lives in a visually separate grown-up band or on the back-matter pages.
    - The child is the hero.
    - A device may be funny or sleepy, never evil.
    - Children who like tech are never mocked.
30. **Read-aloud craft:**
    - A steady meter or a refrain.
    - 40 words or fewer per spread for ages 2–6 (fewer in board books).
    - One joke or hidden detail per spread for the adult.
    - A pause where the child fills in the line.
    - The founder reads every manuscript aloud 3 times, once at a tired bedtime pace.
31. **First-words pictures show one focal object in its most typical current form,** drawn in the brand style. No photos, no dated or brand-like tech, and no phone or tablet as a first word.
32. **Card design:**
    - A big picture icon a 3-year-old can "read".
    - An age-band edge colour **plus** a word label, never colour alone (gate #20).
    - Corner flags: "Can do alone / With a grown-up" and "Nothing needed / Needs: ___". Every card for under-5s is marked "With a grown-up".
    - A talk line on every card.
    - Helper-job cards are never a punishment for saying "I'm bored".
33. **Conversation decks:**
    - No trivia, quiz, right-or-wrong or truth-or-dare cards.
    - Mark each card "light" or "deeper".
    - Include one "How to ask the follow-up" card.
    - One card type per deck.
34. **Stage charts** (course and guide) are organised by stage, never by age deadlines.
    - Never use "late talker", "speech delay", "not talking" or "catch up".
    - Never give milestone ranges or "is this normal?" framing.
    - Every stage page says: "Every child talks on their own timeline. If you have questions about your child's development, talk with your pediatrician."
35. **Inclusive cast in every series** (BRAND-RESPECT-PLAN move 5):
    - children who sign, point or use AAC, and children with glasses, hearing aids, a wheelchair or a limb difference;
    - varied family structures, with dads and grandparents as everyday caregivers;
    - a cast bible so the cast stays consistent.

### Listings and honesty
36. **Lead with the number of activities, never raw pages.**
    - Bullet 1 breaks the pages down, for example "148 pages = 120 activity pages + 22 cut sheets + 6 guide pages".
    - Image 2 is a contents grid.
    - Never add Letter and A4 page counts together.

39. **Never promise durations** ("30 minutes of independent play"). Use "often" or "many families find".

    - Never quote a review publicly without re-checking it live and keeping its context.

41. **Any "N games" or "X ways" claim must match the product exactly.**
42. **Subscriptions** show price, interval and renewal date before payment, cancel online in one step, and send a reminder before every renewal (LEGAL-LAUNCH-CHECKLIST #17).
43. **Courses** include:
    - age-band boxes in every module;
    - a siblings, twins and multi-age box in every module;
    - a searchable FAQ;
    - automatic check-ins on days 3, 10 and 30;
    - a one-page summary per module.
    Never promise personal replies.

### Print and physical
44. **Pages a child draws, colours or dot-markers on are single-sided** in KDP interiors, and the listing says "crayons recommended". Test the proof with crayon, washable marker and dot marker.
45. **Get a physical proof from every printer before listing.**
    - Design for POD: saturated mid-tones, no large dark fields, no fine gradients.
    - Story text is at least 18 pt on 8.5-inch trims.
    - Say "paperback" plainly in listings.
46. **POD decks** use:
    - the heaviest matte or linen stock the provider offers (310–330 gsm or more [VERIFY]);
    - rounded corners;
    - a custom printed tuck box;
    - a how-to-play card.
    Drop-, bend- and spill-test the proof. Make a POD deck only after the PDF deck sells.

47. **Written QA before any file ships:**

    - count every object against its caption;
    - check characters against the SVG symbols;
    - check every age label;
