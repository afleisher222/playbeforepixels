# Community editions: products for every age group and community

Status as of September 28, 2026. Founder request: "make sure we have products for all age groups, types of communities that would be profitable."

**What this is.** Ideas for community editions, each scored on profit, brand fit and effort. Almost every item reuses existing art and content (`visual-routine-cards`, `bored-play-cards`, `play-talk-cards`, `guide-100-plays`, `board-up-go-more`, `picture-laps-not-apps`), so a new edition is mostly a re-sort, relayout or translation, not a new product line. Near-duplicates were merged, and anything that broke a brand rule or could not run without daily work was cut (see the cut list at the end).

**Nothing here is proven demand.** Every price and every demand claim is marked VERIFY and gets checked in the monthly research run (`ops/ROUTINE.md`) before anything is built. Proven demand for a category is not proven demand for our listing (see `marketing/DEMAND-CHECK.md`). The first five launch products in `ops/QUEUE.md` come first, and every edition of a launch product waits until the English base files are final.

**Rules that apply to every item below** (on top of `brand/BRAND.md` hard rules 1-7):
- **Rule 4 child safety** on every play: adult supervision; nothing that fits through a toilet-paper tube for under-3s; no balloons under 8; no neck-length cords or strings; water play always supervised; no choking-hazard foods for toddlers.
- **Targeting rule** (Autism searches, binding): no autism, diagnosis, therapy, "speech delay", "nonverbal" or clinical keywords in any language, and no product is marketed to a community defined by disability or diagnosis.
- **Target the situation, never the status.** Copy never names or assumes income, immigration status, religion, custody, divorce, why a child lives with a caregiver, or how a family was formed. We never collect buyer data on religion, country of origin, immigration status, service status or anything about a child in care.
- **Outreach exclusions:** Montgomery County Public Schools, MCEA/MSEA/NEA, Montgomery County DHHS and its Infants and Toddlers Program, and every other Montgomery County body are never contacted, never listed as targets, and are auto-declined in the license quote form.
- **Held items** (school-facing, co-op, provider or employment-adjacent) stay held until employment counsel answers in writing.
- **Self-running:** promotion is organic pins, SEO articles and the email list only. No posting into groups, no ads targeted by status, no live events, no manual daily tasks.
- **Every product leads to the next and ships with an Amazon plan:** each `listing.json` carries `next_products`, `bonus_url` and `amazon_route`.
- **DEMAND-CHECK pricing rules:** a count in the title (rule 1), list price with a 30-40% sale (rule 2), never a single page under $5 (rule 3), one listing for many searches (rule 5), printable first and print-on-demand deck only after it sells (rule 8).
- **Reviewers** (translators, faith reviewers, a Deaf signer) are paid, work asynchronously by written email, sign off in writing and are credited if they agree.
- **The founder rewrites or approves all copy** before copyright registration (human authorship rule).
