Hi there,

Today's idea: **say what you see.**

When your child is busy, put a few words right on top of what they're doing:

- "Big splash!"
- "You found the red cup."
- "Up, up… it fell!"

No questions to answer, no right or wrong. You're simply adding words to the moment your child already cares about. If they add a sound or a word, add one word back: "Car!" … "Blue car!"

It works anywhere: the bath, the kitchen floor, the window, the walk to the car.
