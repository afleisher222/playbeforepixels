**Subject:** The one-second trick in every play
**Preview text:** Say a little, then wait.

Hi there,

Here's the smallest idea in every play we make: **the pause.**

Say a little, then stop. Count to five in your head. Wait with a friendly face.

"Ready, set…" (wait) "…go!"

That quiet moment is your child's turn. A look, a wiggle, a sound, a reach or a word are all answers, and you can answer them right back. That's a conversation, even before there are many words in it.

Try it today in any play: before the tower crashes, before the blanket drops, before the next spoonful.
