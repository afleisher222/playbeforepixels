## How the ranking works

- **Order:** Fit (5 = best) first, then scope, national first. Ties go to confirmed rows, then to groups with state affiliates or chapters, then to groups with a faceless channel.
- **Confirmed = N** means a site, date, price or reach method could not be checked (live web access was blocked during verification). Verify before spending. Almost no exhibit prices are published, so request the prospectus or media kit first. A DNS check on Sep 28, 2026 resolved 223 of 225 domains; the two failures are flagged in the workbook.
- **Faceless only:** the founder never appears on camera, presents or staffs a booth. Use ads, directories and marketplaces, member discounts, resource listings, written articles, mailed or emailed outreach, and booths staffed by a hired rep.
- **Outreach order:** (1) fit-5 national orgs with state affiliates, NAEYC Affiliate Network and NAFCC; (2) the other fit-5 national channels; (3) fit-5 home-state groups; (4) fit-4 national, faceless channels first; (5) everything else. International targets wait until a localized edition exists.
- **Exclusions:** never contact or target anything on the workbook's Excluded tab. If an event or list includes people from an excluded organization, do not approach them.

## Top 25

1. **NAEYC Affiliate Network (state and local AEYCs)** (Fit 5 · national · Confirmed Y): About 50 state affiliates plus local chapters run conferences, PD and newsletters; likely the cheapest, highest-return exhibit and newsletter channel for ECE educators. **First step:** Use NAEYC's Affiliate Event Calendar to list 2026–27 state conferences; request vendor-table and newsletter-ad rates from MD, DC, VA, PA and DE first.
2. **National Association for Family Child Care (NAFCC)** (Fit 5 · national · Confirmed Y): Home-based providers buy their own materials; 1,300+ conference attendees, 30,000+ promo contacts, and state FCC associations are listed through NAFCC. **First step:** Email conference@nafcc.org for the Jul 15–17, 2027 Jacksonville sponsor/exhibitor package and propose a member-discount partnership.
3. **Independent Book Publishers Association (IBPA)** (Fit 5 · national · Confirmed Y): IBPA staffs its cooperative ALA Annual booth, the cheapest faceless way into the ALA exhibit hall; it also runs member SLJ/LJ ad programs. **First step:** Join IBPA (about $155/yr) and reserve an ALA Annual 2027 full-shelf display ($725 member) as soon as 2027 spots open (the 2026 single-title spots sold out).
4. **PTO Today (School Family Media)** (Fit 5 · national · Confirmed Y): Mailed to 84,000+ K–8 parent groups with 55,000+ newsletter subscribers, the exact buyers of the fundraiser and workshop kits; the directory and ads need no staff. **First step:** Email jwilliams@ptotoday.com (800-644-3561 x201) for current rate cards; start with a Vendor Directory listing plus one Leader Lowdown ad.
5. **WECAN – Waldorf Early Childhood Association of North America** (Fit 5 · national · Confirmed Y): Waldorf early childhood is screen-light and play-based, the closest values match on the list; its 2027 conference page confirms vendors. **First step:** Email conference@waldorfearlychildhood.org about a vendor table and vendor policy for Mar 19–21, 2027 (Chestnut Ridge NY); lead with the play guide and family bundles.
6. **PACEY (Professional Association for Childcare and Early Years)** (Fit 5 · international · Confirmed N): UK childminders buy their own resources; member offers and magazine ads are faceless (unverified; needs a UK/EYFS edition). **First step:** Open pacey.org.uk to confirm current status, then request the media kit and partner-benefit terms and ask whether overseas partners are accepted.
