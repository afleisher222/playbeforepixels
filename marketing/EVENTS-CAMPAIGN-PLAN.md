## How this plan works

- **Every routine run:** read this file and the calendar. Start any row whose **prep start date** falls in the next 7 days, and any row still marked "Planned" whose prep start date has already passed. For each campaign, build:
  - the gift-guide page
  - the bundle
  - the email
  - the faceless posts and pins
  - the POD cutoff notice where printed items are involved
  Then set the row's Status. Paid placements go to `ops/APPROVALS.md` first. Nothing is booked or paid without the founder's verified APPROVED.
- **Seasonal layer, not a second homepage.** The Campaign Bible's lead campaign for the month keeps the site's one primary button. Events here run as the seasonal layer: a gift-guide page one click deep, a store collection, emails and pins. At most one seasonal slot appears on the homepage (calm-site rule).
- **Lead times:**
  - 6 weeks for printables, content and collections.
  - 8 weeks when a print-on-demand (POD) gift is featured.
  - 10 weeks for the spring screen-free week.
  - Gifting rows use each moment's "live by" date minus about 2 weeks of build time.
  - Order-by notices start 14 days before each POD cutoff.
- **Cadence (all year):**
  - 1 seasonal email a week (plus the flagship sequences below).
  - 3–5 pins a day.
  - Seasonal pins go up 4 weeks before a printable moment and 8 weeks before a printed gift.
  - The "instant download" message runs in the final week.
  - A first-Saturday outdoor-play post goes up every month. Build all 12 once, now.

### Rules that apply to every campaign

1. **Mandatory exclusions.** Never target, sponsor, advertise with or reference:
   - Montgomery County Public Schools
   - MCEA, MSEA or NEA, including NEA's "Read Across America". Post nothing on March 2. No author-birthday or "across America" wording.
   - Montgomery County DHHS or its Infants and Toddlers Program.

   Also:
   - No pitching, attending or lead collection at Maryland or DC venues.
   - Geo-exclude Montgomery County (MD) from any digital ad tied to a nearby event.
   - US teacher-appreciation week and National Teacher Day are **removed** because NEA co-runs them.
2. **Faceless and no-contact.** The founder never attends, speaks, calls or appears on camera. Participation means:
   - gift-guide pages, bundles and emails
   - illustrated carousels, pins and animated-text videos
   - inserts shipped straight from the printer
   - cooperative displays staffed by someone else
   - capped ads

   Organizers are contacted by email only, on written terms.
3. **Owned names.** Never use another organization's campaign or event name as our brand, product title or logo. Use the calendar's "Public wording (generic)" column. Examples:
   - "spring screen-free week"
   - "the March unplugging day"
   - "November family reading month"
   - "book day in March"
   - "the April early-childhood week"
   - "shop-small weekend"
4. **Brand rules.**
   - No health or medical claims. This covers mud play, handwriting and digital-wellness days too.
   - Never name or criticize a school, district, company, show or EdTech product. Show only a neutral, generic device.
   - Nothing is timed to autism or acceptance observances.
   - Allowed citations only.
   - No fear words.
   - Honest pricing: real, time-limited promotions only; no countdown timers; no fake "was" prices.
   - The card deck is pitched "for kids" only after its CPSIA status is confirmed.
5. **Gates.**
   - School- and educator-facing items (licensed group kits, classroom angles, educator conferences) are **ON HOLD** until counsel answers. Use the parent/home angle only.
   - The **school-age family plan** is a 5–12 parent product ([G1]). Where it appears below, fall back to the Play-First Family Kit until counsel clears 5–12 products.
   - Press and gift-guide media pitches wait for counsel, per the playbook.
