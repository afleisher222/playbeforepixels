# Amazon everywhere, then big-box retail (founder goal, September 28, 2026)

All facts below are from general knowledge; items marked [VERIFY] are re-checked in the fresh-session verification pass and the monthly research run before any money is spent.

## A. Every product on Amazon, in the form Amazon allows
| Product type | Amazon route | Inventory? | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Picture books, parent books, course book | KDP paperback (+ hardcover where page count allows; IngramSpark for 32-page square hardcovers) | No (print on demand) | Default for every book |
| Printables (busy book, routine cards, bored cards, Family Kit, planners, first-phone plan) | **KDP paperback activity-book edition** of each (cut-out pages, write-in planners); the PDF stays on Etsy/our site | No | Add a KDP edition to every printable where it works on paper |
| Adult tees | Amazon Merch on Demand (application required; tiered upload limits) [VERIFY] | No | Apply after the first designs clear originality |
| Card decks, board books, boxed sets | Amazon FBA (stock held and shipped by Amazon) or Seller Central with a 3PL | Yes — held by Amazon/3PL, never the founder | Wave 3, only after a product proves demand; CPSIA tracking labels/CPC for children's products [VERIFY] |
| Kindle ebooks | KDP (fixed-layout for picture books) | No | Optional per title |
| Amazon Author Central + Brand Registry (after trademark filing) | Author page per marketplace; A+ content; brand protection | — | After launch / after filing |

**Rule (added to the routine):** every new product ships with an Amazon plan in its listing.json (`amazon_route`: kdp-paperback / kdp-activity-edition / merch-on-demand / fba-later / none-with-reason).

## B. Road to Target, Walmart and other stores (milestones, not promises)
1. **Build proof online (months 0–12):** sales history, reviews (FTC-compliant), consistent branding, a strong Amazon presence.
2. **Bookstores and libraries:** IngramSpark distribution (wholesale discount and returnability chosen per title), Bookshop.org, library jobbers (Follett, Mackin) [VERIFY current terms].
3. **Online big-box marketplaces:** Walmart Marketplace (application; approval criteria) and Target Plus (invitation-only) [VERIFY].
4. **Wholesale to boutiques and gift shops:** Faire — only once offset stock sits at a 3PL (wholesale pays about half of retail).
5. **Retail readiness checklist (before pitching any chain):** GS1 barcodes (UPC/GTIN) owned by AlphaPlay LLC, retail-ready packaging, product liability insurance at retailer limits, CPSIA testing and certificates for children's products, EDI capability through a 3PL, a distributor or sales rep, and a 12-month sales record.
6. **Books into Target/Walmart:** usually through book distributors and wholesalers rather than direct vendor setup; a traditional or hybrid publishing or distribution deal for a proven title is the common path [VERIFY].
7. **Pitch:** only with proof — sell-through data, review counts, and a line (series + bundles) a buyer can place on a shelf.

The monthly research run tracks progress against these milestones and adds the next step to ops/QUEUE.md when a milestone is reached.
