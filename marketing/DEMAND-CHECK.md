# Demand check: which Play Before Pixels products will actually sell

Status as of September 28, 2026. It covers every planned product (books, parent printables, teacher resources, card decks, merch and kits) and the proven bestseller formats we don't have yet.

**How the evidence was gathered, and its limits.** The network proxy blocked direct pages on Amazon, Etsy, Teachers Pay Teachers (TPT), Target, B&N and Goodreads. So every figure below comes from search-result snippets captured on September 27-28, 2026. Many Etsy review counts in snippets are **shop-level, not listing-level**. TPT does not show sales publicly. Sales-per-day figures are **estimates** and are labelled that way. Prices marked "est." were not seen on a live listing. **Re-check every figure marked [VERIFY] on the live page before pricing is final.**

**What "demand" means here.** Strong = several competitors with large sales, review counts, Bestseller badges or a visible rank. Moderate = several competitors and clear price points, but little volume data. Weak = few competitors with no sales signal, or strong free alternatives. Unclear = no usable data.

**One honest warning.** Proven demand for a *category* is not proven demand for *our* listing. A new, faceless shop with zero reviews starts behind incumbents that have 2,000-11,000 reviews. We also refuse the keywords that power many of those incumbents ("speech therapy", "SLP", "autism", "ADHD", "therapy"). That is correct under brand rule 1, but it lowers our ranking ceiling. We have to win on design, age-banding, the "talk while you play" line and bundle value. No product here is guaranteed to sell well. The list is built so that nothing weak takes a listing slot of its own.

**Launch constraint carried over from marketing/BLIND-SPOTS.md.** Anything school-facing (TPT, PTA and school kits) stays **on hold until employment counsel answers**. Those products are scored on their evidence below, but none of them is in the first-launch five.

## 4. Pricing and bundle rules learned from the top sellers

1. **Put a big number in the title.** Winners lead with a count (129, 135+, 150+, 200+, 365, 740+). Our titles should too: "150 Screen-Free Play Cards", "200+ Routine Cards".
2. ~~**List at an anchor price, then sell at 30-40% off.**~~ **SUPERSEDED BY BRAND.md "Honest pricing": a permanent discount off an anchor price is a deceptive former-price claim (16 CFR 233.1). Set everyday prices where buyers expect them; run only genuine, time-limited promotions.** This is the Etsy norm (for example $17.75 listed and sold at $8.87, or $8.57 minus 35%). Set list prices so the sale price lands where the buyer expects it.
3. **Never list a single page under $5.** Single printables race toward $1-3 ($1.48 was seen). Weak single pages (media plan, 30-day tracker, bucket list, chore chart) only appear inside bundles.
4. **Build a ladder: single, then seasonal add-on, then complete bundle** at 10-25% off the sum of its parts (the 3rd Grade Thoughts ladder of $6, $13.50 and $35.55). New seasonal packs are added to the bundle automatically.
5. **One listing, many searches.** Merge related formats (road trip + restaurant + waiting room) so one listing ranks for all of them instead of three weak listings.
6. **Match the expected file features:** editable in free Acrobat or Canva, US Letter and A4, Monday and Sunday starts, several colorways, pre-filled and blank versions, and lamination and velcro tips. For TPT, include print plus a teacher-projected version.
7. **Seasonal timing drives badges.** List summer products in March-April, winter indoor and holiday travel products in late October, New Year reset products in December, and spring screen-free week products in March.
8. **Digital first, physical second.** Test every deck or physical format as a printable first. Make the print-on-demand version only after the printable sells.
9. **Physical price bands:** board book $12.99 (between the discounted $7.52 of traditional publishers and the $15.99 of SLP self-publishers); 3-pack $29.99 marked "save"; personalized gift book $34.99; landed unit cost at or below 35-40% of retail.
10. **TPT pricing floor of $5.** Basic TPT sellers keep 55% minus $0.30 per sale (a $3 item nets about $1.35) [VERIFY], so price singles at $5 or more and push bundles.
11. **Free samplers are lead magnets, not lost sales.** A 10-card sampler on TPT, a 30-day tracker and research brief on our site. Each one collects emails.
