# Play Before Pixels: Marketing Playbook

How to reach schools, teachers, parents, organizations and buyers worldwide. It works with no founder face, no personal following, no calls and no live events.

- **Status:** September 28, 2026. Built only from the five reviewed segment plans (schools and districts, teachers, parents, organizations, international and always-on).
- **Owner:** AlphaPlay LLC, doing business as Play Before Pixels.
- **Budget assumed:** about 5 founder hours a week. Claude Code routines draft the work. The founder approves in `ops/APPROVALS.md`.
- **Confirmed** means the segment reviewers checked it against a current source. **[UNVERIFIED]** means check it live on the day you sign up or spend money. Prices and platform rules change often.

> **Read this first: three gates that override everything below**
> 1. **Employment counsel gate.** Anything school-facing or group-facing stays built but unpublished until employment counsel answers `legal/FOR-EMPLOYMENT-COUNSEL.md` Q1–Q3, Q4–Q6, Q10 and Q11 (see `marketing/BLIND-SPOTS.md` item 1). That covers the Schools page, TPT/Tes, teacher and PTA email, library and child-care pitches, the fundraiser and press. Until counsel answers, launch only the parent products (0–5 first). Two questions only counsel can answer: whether she may sell to her employer's families or colleagues at all, and whether she may sell to neighboring districts.
> 2. **Mailing address.** The task brief asked for the address line "11140 Rockville Pike, Suite 100-559". `legal/ENTITY.md` (founder decision, Sept 28, 2026) says she **no longer uses that mailbox**. A CAN-SPAM footer must carry a valid postal address, so every template here uses `AlphaPlay LLC, [BUSINESS MAILING ADDRESS]`. That placeholder is the USPS PO Box she will rent. Rent the box before any marketing email goes out. Never use the home address.
> 3. **No direct contact** (`brand/BRAND.md`, Sept 28, 2026). No phone number, calls, video, interviews, podcasts or in-person events. Buyers reach the business only by contact form and email. That is why conference tactics below are limited to inserts, ads and cooperative displays. Podcast guesting is not used, even audio-only.

## Segment 1: Schools and districts

Covers public districts (never the excluded employer), private and independent schools, early-learning centers, school libraries, and homeschool co-ops and microschools. US first; the same assets work worldwide.

### Who buys and why
| Buyer | How they pay | What they want |

| Family-engagement and Title I coordinators, principals | PO; parent-and-family-engagement (PFE) funds | Turnkey family-night content and loaner book sets. | Independent-school heads, center directors, microschool and co-op leaders | Card or simple invoice; ESA platforms (ClassWallet in Arizona; Odyssey in Iowa, Louisiana, Utah, Texas, Wyoming, Georgia) | A fast, simple purchase |

**The pitch:** turnkey, print-based, play-first resources with no student accounts or logins. State that as a plain fact about the product, never as a comparison with any other product. Talk about play, language and connection, never health.

**Money facts.** The federal micro-purchase threshold is $15,000 as of Oct 1, 2025, and a district may self-certify up to $50,000 (confirmed, 2 CFR 200.320). This applies **only to purchases charged to federal awards**. State and local money follows state bid law and board policy. So never write "no bid needed". Write "priced for small-purchase procedures; confirm with your purchasing office."

### Where they look
- Titlewave, Mackin, Booklist, School Library Journal, Kirkus, and BlueInk's monthly favorites list.
- Junior Library Guild, through the Media Source Inc. portal.
- Educator Google searches, TPT, Pinterest.
- State education agency Title I family-engagement guidance pages.
- BidNet Direct: free, with 100+ districts posting bids.
- The National Microschooling Center's Access Point catalog and its Partnership Provider Program (P3).
- ESA marketplaces.
- Baker & Taylor stopped operating in January 2026 (confirmed). Never plan around it.

### Lead offer
PreK–5 Screen-Free Classroom Pack site license, with a same-day written quote and a W-9 on request. For family engagement: the Family-Night Host Kit plus a loaner book set, billed as one PO line.

- Federally funded nonprofits follow the Uniform Guidance, where the micro-purchase threshold is $15,000.
- Pediatric practices that bill Medicare or Medicaid follow OIG's nominal-value line: $15 per item and $75 per patient per year (confirmed).

## Cold-email rules, applied to every template

1. Build lists only from public role addresses that organizations published themselves. **Never** buy consumer lists, scrape LinkedIn or text anyone.
2. Run the suppression check and log it before every batch.
3. Send from the business domain, never personal Gmail or work email. Plain text. 20–40 a day. 3 touches at most.
4. **Every** message has:
   - an accurate From line ("Play Before Pixels team")
   - an honest subject
   - a line saying it is a promotional message
   - `AlphaPlay LLC, [BUSINESS MAILING ADDRESS]`
   - a one-step opt-out that asks for nothing but the email address, works for at least 30 days and is honored within 10 business days (FTC CAN-SPAM guide, confirmed)
   - no phone number.
5. Never sell or transfer addresses.
6. **Canada (CASL):** email only addresses the organization published, with no "no marketing" note, and only about something relevant to the recipient's role.
7. **UK (PECR):** companies and public bodies are fine; sole traders need consent.
8. **EU:** don't cold email.
9. Respect any posted no-solicitation policy.
10. The founder approves each batch in `ops/APPROVALS.md`.

## Never do (the hard rules)

1. **No health or medical claims.** Never write "prevents speech delay", "improves attention", "boosts brain development", "reduces ADHD", "therapy", "cure" or "clinically proven". The founder is **not an SLP**; never imply clinical authorship. Research is cited neutrally, using only the citations in BRAND.md, as associations, never causes, with "not medical advice".
2. **Never name, rate, compare or criticize** any school, district, company, app or EdTech product. That includes "unlike [app]" lines, and writing "no logins" as a jab. No local angle.
3. **Never contact, list or target the excluded employer:** Montgomery County Public Schools (MD), its schools, staff, families and PTAs; MCEA, MSEA and NEA; Montgomery County DHHS and its Infants and Toddlers Program. Also never contact her colleagues or anyone she knows through work. Never use work time, devices, email, curriculum or logos. Selling to her employer, its families or colleagues is **a question for her employment counsel**.
4. **Lawful outreach only.** Every commercial email meets CAN-SPAM (valid postal address, clear opt-out honored within 10 business days, honest headers, identified as promotional). No purchased consumer lists. No texting without prior express written consent. No LinkedIn scraping. No bots filling in contact forms. Check CASL and PECR; no EU cold email.
5. **Ads follow platform rules.** No health-condition, pregnancy, religion or personal-attribute targeting or copy on Meta, Google or Pinterest. No "Is your child…?" copy. No custom audiences built from lists without consent. No autism keywords anywhere.
6. **Faceless and no direct contact.** No founder photo, video, voice-on-video, personal profile, calls, interviews, podcasts or live events. Workshops exist only as kits someone else presents.
7. **No incentivized, fake or AI-written reviews.** No review gating. Never tie a freebie or discount to a review.
8. **No false procurement claims.** No sole-source claim for anything sold elsewhere. Never "Title I approved". Never "no bid needed".
9. **No implied endorsements.** Never "PTA-approved". Never use a program's trademark ("1000 Books Before Kindergarten") or logo. No Screen-Free Week logo or claimed affiliation.
10. **No inventory at home** and no board-book or kids'-deck marketing before CPSIA compliance. No home address on anything public.
11. **No referral fees or gifts** to clinicians, practices or public employees in return for recommendations or purchases.
12. **Nothing goes out without approval** in `ops/APPROVALS.md`.
