## Play Before Pixels for classrooms
Play Before Pixels makes print-based, play-first resources for PreK–5 classrooms and families: talk-along picture books, a 52-card play deck, the *100 Plays* guide, a PreK–5 Screen-Free Classroom Pack, and host-it-yourself Family Night kits.

- **No student accounts, logins or devices are needed.**
- **We collect no student data.**
- These are educational materials, not clinical, therapeutic or speech-language services.

Play Before Pixels is a trade name of **AlphaPlay LLC**.

## A note on purchasing rules
Our license and kit prices are set to fit small-purchase procedures. Purchasing rules depend on your funding source, your state and your district's policy, so **please confirm with your purchasing office.**

**Family-engagement funds:** some state education agencies list instructional kits and books for loan programs as possible allowable family-engagement costs. For example, the New York State Education Department's Title I, Part A parent-and-family-engagement guidance does. **Your district's Title I office decides allowability.** We never describe any product as "approved" for a funding program.

## License terms, in plain English
- **Single Classroom:** one teacher may print and use the materials with their own class, year after year.
- **Grade-Band / Site:** every teacher in the covered grades at one school site may print and use them.
- **District:** the sites named on the license.
- **All licenses:** no resale, no posting on public websites, and please keep the copyright line on every page.
