# Pediatric practice and hospital new-parent program email (2 versions)

**Audience:** practice managers of pediatric and family practices, and hospital foundation, community-benefit and family-education staff. Find them through **national** directories and public pages only, never local Montgomery County ones (BLIND-SPOTS item 15).
**Gate:**
- Version A (a free printable, about 5 a week) may start before counsel answers.
- Version B (paid bulk kit) waits for counsel.

Never contact Montgomery County DHHS or its Infants and Toddlers Program.

**Hard rules for this audience:**
- **No referral fees, commissions, discounts or gifts** to practices or clinicians for recommending anything. **Never ask anyone to "prescribe" or recommend** a product.
- **No health, medical or developmental claims.** Nothing implies clinician or SLP authorship. The founder is not an SLP.
- The retail value of the per-family giveaway stays **under $15**, per OIG's nominal-value guidance for practices that bill Medicare or Medicaid. The practice's compliance office decides.
- Sell at arm's length, on invoice only.
