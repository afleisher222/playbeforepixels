# Outreach templates: how to use them

Every file here is a draft. Nothing is sent, posted or published until the founder approves it in `ops/APPROVALS.md`. The rules are in `../MARKETING-PLAYBOOK.md`, which you should read first.

## Placeholders
- `[BUSINESS MAILING ADDRESS]` is the USPS PO Box from `legal/ENTITY.md`. **Don't use the old 11140 Rockville Pike mailbox.** The founder no longer uses it (Sept 28, 2026), and a CAN-SPAM footer must carry a valid address. Never use the home address. No marketing email goes out until the box is rented.
- `[BUSINESS DOMAIN]` is the brand email domain, for example `hello@` and `orders@`. Never use personal Gmail or work email.
- `[UNSUBSCRIBE LINK]` is the email tool's one-click unsubscribe. A plain-text "reply unsubscribe" also works.
- `[First name]`, `[Organization]`, `[LINK: …]` and `$__` get filled in per send. `$__` means research didn't set a price. Set it after the landed-cost check (never below about 2× landed cost).
- Never add a phone number, and never offer a call, meeting, interview or video. Written channels only (BRAND.md).

## Before every cold-email batch (paste into the approval request)
- [ ] Every address is a role address the organization published itself. No purchased or scraped lists.
- [ ] Suppression check run and logged: `mcpsmd.org`, `mcpsmd.net`, `montgomeryschoolsmd.org` and any subdomain of these; every MCPS school and program, MCPS school PTAs and their council; MCEA, MSEA and NEA; Montgomery County DHHS, including Infants and Toddlers and its child care resource center; the Montgomery County, MD buffer list; opt-outs; bounces; anyone she knows through work.
- [ ] Canada: the address was published by the recipient with no "no marketing" note, and the message is relevant to the recipient's role (CASL). UK: not a sole trader (PECR). EU: don't send.
- [ ] The footer below is present, and nothing in the email names or criticizes a school, district, company or product or makes a health claim.
- [ ] Batch size of 20–40 per day, sent from the business domain.

## Standard footer (goes in every cold email)
```

This is a promotional message from Play Before Pixels, a trade name of AlphaPlay LLC.
AlphaPlay LLC, [BUSINESS MAILING ADDRESS]
Don't want these emails? Reply "unsubscribe" or click [UNSUBSCRIBE LINK], and we'll remove this address within 10 business days (usually the same week). We never sell or share addresses.
Play Before Pixels offers educational materials, not medical, therapy or speech-language advice.
```
