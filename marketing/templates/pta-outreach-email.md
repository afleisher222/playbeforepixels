# PTA/PTO outreach email (3 touches)

**Audience:** PTA/PTO presidents, fundraising chairs and family-engagement chairs of non-excluded schools. Where possible, use the group's official address or its public web contact form, not an officer's personal email.
**Gate:** after employment counsel clears group-facing sales. **Never** send to MCPS school PTAs or the council serving them. Also hold all Montgomery County, MD groups and Maryland PTA state-level contacts until counsel clears them.
**Timing:** Jan–May (boards plan next year and vote on vendors) and Aug–Sept (fall events).
**Rules:** Never write "PTA-approved" or "endorsed". Use no PTA's or officer's name in any public material. Before promising a payout, confirm the fundraiser percentage with the per-format margin check and counsel's co-venturer answer (see `fundraiser-program-one-pager.md`).
