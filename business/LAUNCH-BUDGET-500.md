## What waits until sales pay for it

- Paid ads: the stress test shows they lose money at launch prices anyway (business/STRESS-TEST.md).
- Paid reviewers for the research pages: about $4,900. The research pages stay unpublished until then; the shop doesn't need them.
- Attorney review of the policy drafts: use the drafts marked DRAFT for the first sales only if counsel agrees. Otherwise this is the first thing the buffer or the first profits pay for.
- Extra domains, Shopify ($39/month), print-on-demand merch samples, the IngramSpark hardcover, ISBNs you buy yourself (you chose Amazon's free ISBN).
- Social platforms other than Pinterest: free, but they wait until February on purpose.

## Watch for

- **Maryland annual report:** about $300, due each April 15. If this year's is unpaid, the LLC may not be in good standing, and that comes before anything else. Check at Maryland Business Express.
- **Employment counsel:** the written yes on selling is the one gate. Its cost is between you and your lawyer and is not part of this budget.

## What Claude does for $0 in the cloud

After the accounts exist and their keys are in the environment, Claude does all of this:
- builds and updates every listing, image, price and bundle;
- deploys and fixes the website;
- pins every day, and sends the welcome and monthly emails;
- does the monthly books for your accountant;
- researches weekly and builds a new product daily.

It runs on your Max plan, overnight. **Uploads to KDP and Teachers Pay Teachers** have no automation, so for those Claude prepares a ready packet and you click upload.
