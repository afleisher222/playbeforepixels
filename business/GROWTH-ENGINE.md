## 1. For the founder, in five lines

1. **The strategy.** When counsel says yes in writing, sell ages 0–5 printables on Etsy and Gumroad and one grown-up paperback on Amazon. After that, growth comes from bundles, new editions of whatever sells, free Pinterest pins and an opt-in email list, not ads. At today's prices, $150 a month of ads *lowers* median profit by $721 (S: `business/STRESS-TEST.md` §6).
2. **Your part.** About 4.5 hours of one-time setup over the next two weeks: the counsel email today, then the bank, insurance, domain and one 75-minute account sitting (A). After that, about 10–15 hours of proofs and approvals through January (A).
3. **Dates.** Ask counsel today for an answer by Fri Oct 16. If the answer is yes and the rest of Gate A holds (§2b), that is launch day. The targets after that are: first sale within 7 days, paperback live Oct 23–30, Black Friday Nov 27, *30 Days of Back-and-Forth* on Dec 26, and checkpoints on Dec 31 and Mar 31 (A; Black Friday S: task brief).
4. **Money, honestly.** A middle-of-the-road first year is about $6,700 of sales and a $3,100 operating loss. The plan's Expected year ($11,946) happens in only about one future in four (S: STRESS-TEST, re-run Sep 29 at the $9.50 routine-card price). With an Oct 16 G-day, the Expected case reaches its first $1,000 month in about March 2027. That is January only if October counts as a full selling month, and April with a December first sale. The median future runs close to Conservative, which gets there between about June 2027 (a full October of sales) and October 2027 (a November or December first sale) (A: workbook re-run Sep 29; see "Math check"). A $10,000 month first appears in December 2027, and only in the Strong case with sales from October or November. That is roughly the best one future in twenty (S: STRESS-TEST, 5% beat Strong). Its early sign would be about 250 or more orders in March 2027 (A). With a December or later first sale, even Strong waits until November 2028 (S: workbook).
5. **"Exponential," honestly.** From December 2026 to December 2027, Expected grows about 28% a month (S: workbook, December first sale). Nearly all of that is the launch ramp from almost zero. With the seasons taken out, growth is about 6–11% a month in the second half of 2027, about 1.5% a month in 2028 once the 15-product cap binds, and about 0.3% a month in 2029 (A: workbook recalculated). That is an S-curve. It compounds again only while editions that sell keep growing the catalog, reviews build up and the list grows. Launching in October instead of December is worth about $1,700 of median first-year profit, which is more than any ad could add (S: STRESS-TEST §3). It pulls sales forward, but it barely raises the later level: Expected December 2027 is about $3,800–$3,900 with an October G-day, against $3,591 (A: workbook re-run Sep 29).

## 3. Growth loops that compound (ranked)

These rank by contribution to profit over 12–24 months, how hands-off they are, and the evidence behind them. **No double counting (A).** The Expected case already contains two things these loops describe:
- **Catalog growth:** 0.75 new products a month, each selling at the full per-product rate, until the 15-product cap binds in February 2028.
- **Traffic:** about 2,700 own-site sessions a month by Sep 2027 (S: workbook) and about 7,500 visitors a month across all channels (S: STRESS-TEST §2).

A loop's gain counts as *extra* only when it goes beyond those. Otherwise it is part of how the Expected case gets delivered.

### Loop 1 — Editions of proven sellers, and folding the rest into bundles

- **Mechanism.** A listing with 5 or more sales in 30 days triggers these steps, in order:
  1. it goes into a bundle;
  2. a next-age or situation edition with **at least 30% new content**, aimed at a separate search (G0 only);
  3. an adult-directed KDP edition (never a 0–3 activity book before CPSC guidance);
  4. a Spanish edition per product, only after the break-even line has held for 2 months, as an APPROVED purchase, reviewed by a native speaker (never machine translation alone) and run through the same compliance gate.

  New editions are **new listings** in the same Etsy shop, so they benefit from the shop's review total. Reviews are never moved or copied between listings, and a listing with reviews is never rewritten into a different product. A listing that fails the kill rule is added to a bundle as a part and its own listing is then deactivated. It is never edited into the bundle (16 CFR 465.3, U; S: EXPERIMENTS EXP-13; 02 §2.2 principle 7; DEMAND-CHECK §3).
- **Routine.** On Mondays the studio reads 30-day sales for each listing and queues the next step in `ops/QUEUE.md`. It builds at most 1 product a day and publishes at most 5 Etsy listings and 2 KDP titles a week. The kill rule and the bundle fold run weekly.
- **Leading metric.** Live listings with a sale in the last 30 days: **8 or more by Jan 31, and 15 or more by Jun 30, 2027** (A). Share of new listings that sell within 30 days: 50% or more (A).
- **Math.** A mature listing sells 14.8 units a month (Expected) to 30.3 (Strong) across the digital channels (S: 03 §3.2). At the workbook's $8.82 of gross per unit (S) and its 1.31× December season (S), that is about **$170–$350 of December gross per listing**, or about $180–$390 by December 2027 with the model's per-product growth (A). A $10,000 December from printables alone therefore needs about 55 mature listings at Expected velocity, or about 25 at Strong. The numbers are about 44 and 20 if the KDP shelf keeps its usual fifth of December gross (A; KDP is 18–20% of December 2027 in every case, S: workbook).

  The model caps the catalog at 15 (S). Take editions of proven sellers, adding 2 a month, each selling at 40% of a hero's rate, with 70% surviving the kill rule (all A). That is 0.56 full-rate products a month, *less* than the 0.75 the Expected case already assumes. Until the cap binds in February 2028, editions are therefore how the studio keeps up with Expected, not an addition to it. If editions are the only new products, Expected's catalog growth is itself about 25% optimistic, unless new categories also sell or editions beat 40% (EXP-16 decides). Raising the cap to 40 **only for editions** adds, with no double counting, about $0 before Feb 2028, about $370 a month by Sep 2028 and about $1,100 by Sep 2029 (A: recomputed here). Thesis B's figures of $216 by Sep 2027 and $651 by Sep 2028 counted editions on top of the model's own new products from April 2027. Monthly growth of the extra is about 23% in Sep 2028 and about 10% by Mar 2029, and it falls to 0 at the cap of 40.
- **When it overtakes launch sales.** About January 2029 on Expected inputs (A: recomputed). Thesis B's "Jun 2028" was inconsistent with its own path, which was still below $656 in Sep 2028. It comes earlier if the heroes sell at Strong velocity.

### Loop 2 — The marketplace review-and-rank flywheel, focused on the heroes

- **Mechanism.** Orders lead to reviews. Reviews come only from Etsy's own review request, plus one neutral email on day 7 to own-checkout buyers (S: EXPERIMENTS EXP-13). Reviews and sales history raise search placement and conversion, and that brings more organic orders. Volume is focused on the busy book, the routine cards and the two bundles so reviews build up in one place. No incentive of any kind, no review gating, no free or advance review copies (advance-copy services included), and no reviews by the founder, her family or anyone acting for the business (16 CFR 465; COMPLIANCE-GATE 22; S: EXP-13). The day-7 email goes to every own-checkout buyer and carries no offer, coupon, referral link or product promotion. The routine never messages Etsy buyers about reviews and never asks anyone to change one.
- **Routine.** Weekly: a listing that gets views but no sales gets one change (first photo *or* title, EXP-02/03). Also: saved replies; a private fix before any public review reply (ROUTINE §5b), meaning a corrected file or a refund through the platform's own order tools, never conditioned on a review and never with a request to change one; a START HERE file; and no more than 5 files per listing.
