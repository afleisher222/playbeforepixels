## 3. Growth loops that compound (ranked)

These rank by contribution to profit over 12–24 months, how hands-off they are, and the evidence behind them. The Expected "launch cohort" these loops are compared against is about $656 a month of digital sales from the launch five at full ramp (A: derived from the model's per-product rates, Thesis B).

### Loop 1 — Editions of proven sellers, and folding the rest into bundles

- **Mechanism.** A listing with 5 or more sales in 30 days triggers these steps, in order:
  1. it goes into a bundle;
  2. a next-age or situation edition with **at least 30% new content**, aimed at a separate search (G0 only);
  3. an adult-directed KDP edition (never a 0–3 activity book before CPSC guidance);
  4. a Spanish edition per product, only after the break-even line has held for 2 months.

  New editions sit in the same Etsy shop, so they inherit its reviews. A listing that fails the kill rule is folded into a bundle (S: 02 §2.2 principle 7; DEMAND-CHECK §3).
- **Routine.** On Mondays the studio reads 30-day sales for each listing and queues the next step in `ops/QUEUE.md`. It builds at most 1 product a day and publishes at most 5 Etsy listings and 2 KDP titles a week. The kill rule and the bundle fold run weekly.
- **Leading metric.** Live listings with a sale in the last 30 days: **8 or more by Jan 31, and 15 or more by Jun 30, 2027** (A). Share of new listings that sell within 30 days: 50% or more (A).
- **Math.** A mature listing sells 14.8 units a month (Expected) to 30.3 (Strong) across the digital channels (S: 03 §3.2). At about $11 a unit (A) and a 1.3× December lift (A; derived from the model), that is about **$210–$430 of December gross per listing**. A $10,000 December therefore needs about 47 mature listings at Expected velocity, or about 23 at Strong (A).

  The model caps the catalog at 15 (S). Raising the cap to 40 **only for editions of proven sellers**, adding 2 a month, each selling at 40% of a hero's rate, with 70% surviving the kill rule (all A), adds about $216 a month by Sep 2027 and about $651 by Sep 2028 (A: Thesis B simulation). Monthly growth is about 30% in Sep 2027, falls to about 5% by Sep 2028, and reaches 0 at the cap.
- **When it overtakes launch sales.** About Jun 2028 on Expected inputs (A). Earlier if the heroes sell at Strong velocity.

### Loop 2 — The marketplace review-and-rank flywheel, focused on the heroes

- **Mechanism.** Orders lead to reviews. Reviews come only from Etsy's own review request, plus one neutral email on day 7 to own-checkout buyers (S: EXPERIMENTS EXP-13). Reviews and sales history raise search placement and conversion, and that brings more organic orders. Volume is focused on the busy book, the routine cards and the two bundles so reviews build up in one place. No incentives, no review gating, and no advance-copy services that penalize readers who don't post (16 CFR 465; COMPLIANCE-GATE 22).
- **Routine.** Weekly: a listing that gets views but no sales gets one change (first photo *or* title, EXP-02/03). Also: saved replies, a private fix before any public review reply (ROUTINE §5b), a START HERE file, and no more than 5 files per listing.
- **Leading metric.** Hero-listing conversion of **2% or more by Jan 31** (A/U). Reviews per 100 orders, with a star average of 4.7 or higher (A).
- **Math.** At a 5% review rate (A/U; plan on 5%, not 15%) and Expected Etsy volumes, the shop has about 23 reviews by Sep 2027 and about 88 by Sep 2028 (A: Thesis B). Incumbents have 2,000–11,000 (S: DEMAND-CHECK). The flywheel pays through conversion: **+20% conversion is worth +$877 of median 12-month profit** (S: STRESS-TEST §6).
- **When it overtakes launch sales.** Never, as a line of its own. It multiplies every other line.

### Loop 3 — The evergreen Pinterest pin library (organic only)

- **Mechanism.** 3–5 illustrated, faceless pins a day across 10–15 keyword boards. **Product pins link to the live Etsy listing**, where conversion is highest and reviews build up. **Free-printable pins link to the email landing page**, which is the only way this traffic can join the list. Seasonal boards are reused every year. Only live products are pinned. No hub pins and no #autism (S: CAMPAIGN-BIBLE [HUB]).
- **Routine.** Daily posting through the API once it is approved, and upload packets until then. Monthly: redesign the top 10% of pins. Each new item gets 5–10 pins. Seasonal pins go up 4 weeks ahead (S: EVENTS).
- **Leading metric.** **100 or more outbound clicks a week by Jan 31** (A). Also track saves per pin and sign-ups by `src=pinterest`.
- **Math.** About 120 new pins a month (S: 3–5 a day). Each pin earns about 1.0 outbound click a month, decaying 6% a month (U). That gives about 450 clicks a month in Mar 2027 and about 920 in Sep 2027, levelling off near 2,860 (A: Thesis B). Monthly growth falls from 19% in mid-2027 to 2.5% by late 2028 (A). **+20% traffic is worth +$881 of median profit** (S: STRESS-TEST §6).
- **When it overtakes launch sales.** Never on its own: about $80 a month in Sep 2027 (A). Its job is traffic, plus about 37 sign-ups a month by Sep 2027 (A).

### Loop 4 — Owned email list, then the stage path, then the January Reset

- **Mechanism.** Emails are collected, from adults only, with the child's birth month and year only, in three places: every non-Etsy edition (the KDP QR code, Gumroad files), every free-printable page, and checkout. Etsy editions carry no URL (S: COMPLIANCE-GATE 16). Each month the "3 plays for your child's age" email walks the family up the stage map (S: 02 §2.6) to the next product. Every January brings the Reset. Hub sign-ups (`src=hub`) receive no product email (S: BRAND "Autism searches").
- **Routine.** The email platform runs the sequences: welcome; after-purchase on days 0, 3, 7 (review), 10 and 30; birthday month; and the monthly issue. The studio drafts each monthly issue from the plays library. The suppression list is checked before every send.
- **Leading metric.**
  - Subscribers: about **110 by Jan 31** (S: 02 §2.3, Expected) and 250 by Apr 30 (A).
  - Opt-in rate above 25% on landing pages (S: MARKETING-PLAYBOOK).
  - Sign-ups per 100 KDP and Gumroad orders: the model assumes 15 (S: 03 §3.2).
  - Revenue per subscriber per month.
- **Math.** About 690 subscribers by Sep 2027 and about 3,500 by month 36 (S: 03 §3.2). The list levels off at inflow ÷ churn, where churn is 1.5% a month (S). The Reset converts 15% of the list reached × 1.5% (S), which is about 2.25 Resets per 1,000 subscribers a month (A). Stage-path promotions at $0.10 per subscriber a month (A/U; range $0.05–0.25) come to about $70 a month by Sep 2027 (A).
- **When it overtakes launch sales.** Never within 36 months at $0.10 a subscriber; about Apr 2028 at $0.25 (A: Thesis B). The payoff comes each January and in years 2–3.

### Loop 5 — Product-led sharing and gift buyers

- **Mechanism.** A share-safe certificate on every product. A caregiver page in own-checkout and KDP editions, added after the license amendment. Gift products that bring new households in: the $29 Instant Gift Bundle with its gift-reveal card, the $5 shower insert and bookplate, the $14 Talk-First Welcome set, and the $12 Play Recipes for Grandparents. See the §4 checklist.
- **Routine.** The §4 checklist runs on every build. Every QR code carries a `src` tag.
- **Leading metric.** QR and bonus sign-ups per 100 own-checkout and KDP orders: **5 or more by week 8** (A). Also track the gift-buyer share of orders.
- **Math.** The blended viral coefficient k is about 0.02–0.06 (A; Thesis C range before the judges' cuts), which multiplies orders by about 1.02–1.06. Since k is well below 1, this amplifies demand but does not create it.
- **When it overtakes launch sales.** Never. Any mechanic with fewer than 0.5 sign-ups per 100 orders after 8 weeks and one redesign is dropped (A).

**Deferred, with no forecast credit: search pages.** There is no `site/` yet (`site-concepts/` belongs to another workflow). Plan 4–6 product-adjacent articles and gift guides on a minimal site once the domain exists. They get credit only after Search Console shows impressions. Research-hub and term pages sit outside this plan: their $4,900 reviewer budget is still PENDING in `ops/APPROVALS.md`.

**What "exponential" means here.**

| When | Expected monthly gross (S: workbook, recalculated in a scratch copy by Thesis A) | Growth rate |
|---|---|---|
| Dec 2026 | $176 | — |
| Apr 2027 | $1,182 | about 28% a month over Dec 2026 → Dec 2027: a launch ramp from almost zero |
| Dec 2027 | $3,364 | — |
| Dec 2028 | $4,135 | about 23% for the whole year, once the 15-product catalog cap binds |

All the loops together, including SEO, grow about 14% a month in mid-2027 (doubling in about 5 months), 7% in late 2028 and 4.5% in 2029 (doubling in about 16 months) (A: Thesis B simulation, so treat this as an upper bound). On conservative inputs the loops never pass launch sales within 36 months. **That is an S-curve.** It keeps compounding only while the catalog keeps growing through editions that sell.
